## R-60 — Neural Controller Architecture, Parameter Budget & Calibration

### 1. Candidate Architectures for `~274d`–`284d` Inputs (`256d` Embedding + `18d`–`28d` Numeric)
| Architecture | Mechanism | Strengths for MonoAgens | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plain MLP** | `Linear -> ReLU/GELU -> Linear` | Simplest Rust forward pass (~60 LOC). | **Underperforms on raw scalar features:** Gorishniy et al. (NeurIPS 2022, [arXiv:2203.05556](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.05556)) **[PR]** showed standard MLPs passing raw scalars into linear layers struggle to capture sharp non-linear thresholds (e.g., `budget_used_ratio > 0.9`). |
| **Residual MLP (Pre-LN + SiLU + Dropout)** | `x + Dropout(Linear(SiLU(Linear(LayerNorm(x)))))` | Gorishniy et al. (NeurIPS 2021, [arXiv:2106.11959](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.11959)) **[PR]** established Pre-LN ResNet-like MLPs as a strong tabular baseline, beating complex attention models when properly regularized. | Still mixes `256d` dense embedding and `18d` scalars at layer 0 unless projected separately. |
| **SwiGLU MLP** | `(SiLU(xW_1) * (xW_3)) W_2` | Shazeer (2020, [arXiv:2002.05202](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.05202)) **[PP]** showed GLU variants (SwiGLU) consistently outperform GELU/ReLU MLPs at matched FLOPs; multiplicative gating naturally models conditional rules (e.g., `is_dyadic * silence_duration`). | 3 weight matrices per block instead of 2 (slightly narrower hidden dim at fixed param budget). |
| **Modality-Fusion + PLR Numeric Embeddings** | `e_proj = LN(W_e e_256)` + `n_proj = PLR(x_num)` → concat/add → Pre-LN ResNet/SwiGLU trunk | **Strong inductive bias for hybrid inputs:** `PLR` (Periodic + Linear + ReLU, Gorishniy et al. 2022 **[PR]**) maps each scalar `x_i` to high-frequency Fourier/piecewise features before fusion, preventing `256d` semantic noise from drowning `18d` structural scalars. | Single point prediction without built-in ensemble epistemic spread. |
| **`TabM` Implicit Ensemble + `PLR`** (Gorishniy et al., ICLR 2025, [arXiv:2410.24210](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.24210) **[PR]**) | Shared MLP/SwiGLU backbone + `k` lightweight BatchEnsemble-style multiplicative adapter vectors (`r_i, s_i`) producing `k` simultaneous predictions | **State-of-the-art 2025 tabular MLP:** beats single MLPs and FT-Transformer at matched compute, AND inter-member variance across the `k` heads supplies **free epistemic uncertainty** directly feeding `p_uncertainty` / abstention. | `k`-way batched matmul (`k=8..16`) slightly multiplies FLOPs (~2–3× vs single forward pass, still sub-ms at 200k params). |

*2025–2026 External Validation of Small Local Triggers:* **Do Proactive Agents Really Need an LLM to Decide When to Wake and What to Anchor?** (Purdue / Microsoft / MSU / GaTech, May 2026, [arXiv:2605.30152](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30152) **[PP]**) directly validates MonoAgens's architecture: a compact local temporal controller (`~1M` params, `13.99 ms` on a consumer laptop) monitoring structured `(actor, verb, object, timestamp)` streams achieved **4–7× speedups** over LLM triggers and improved downstream F1 by **+16.7 points** averaged across **14 distinct LLM backbones**. Related 2025–2026 proactive timing formulations include **Proactive Conversational Agents with Inner Thoughts** (Liu et al., 2025, [arXiv:2501.00383](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00383) **[PP]**), **ProActor** timing-aware RL (May 2026, [arXiv:2605.24900](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24900) **[PP]**), and **Proactive AI: Replannable Dialogue Timelines** (Oct 2026, [arXiv:2610.05159](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05159) **[PP]**).

### 2. Parameter Scaling vs. Dataset Size (`1k` to `100k+` Events)
- **`~50k` params** (e.g., `d_model = 128`, 2 residual blocks): Best when labeled training data is **`1k–10k` events** (e.g., early prototype or single-dataset fine-tune). Low variance, minimal overfitting, ~100 KB `f16` weights.
- **`~200k` params** (e.g., `d_model = 256`, 2–3 SwiGLU/ResNet blocks + PLR): **Recommended sweet spot for `10k–100k` multi-task events.** Enough capacity to learn multi-party turn-taking + memory relevance without memorizing `256d` embedding noise.
- **`~1M` params** (e.g., `d_model = 512`, 3–4 blocks): Justified only at **`50k–100k+` diverse events** across multiple datasets with AdamW weight decay (`1e-2`–`5e-2`) and dropout (`0.1–0.2`).
- **`~3M` params:** High overfitting risk on `274d` frozen-embedding + tabular inputs below `100k+` events; frozen `EmbeddingGemma 2` already compressed the language representation, so a 3M-param head mostly memorizes speaker/corpus artifacts unless heavily regularized. *(Exact crossover point is **unmeasured** until evaluated on MonoAgens's held-out Lab splits.)*

### 3. Core-Boundary-Respecting Output Heads & Calibration
To preserve [`ARCHITECTURE.md:85-87`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/ARCHITECTURE.md#L85-L87) (Core never understands language or overrides Valve), outputs must be **purely control/routing probabilities and scalar hints**:
- `gate_4` logits → `softmax` over `[Silent, Note, Wait, Go]` + `wait_delta_t` (bounded `[0, t_max]`).
- `p_uncertainty` & `p_verify` $\in [0, 1]$ (used as numeric budget/recall-retry hints, never overriding Valve rules).
- `p_encode_importance` $\in [0, 1]$ & `p_memory_relevance` $\in [0, 1]$ (ranking over `VisibleScope`-filtered candidates only).

**Calibration & Selective Prediction Metrics (Updated with 2026 Agentic UQ Standards):**
- **Post-hoc Temperature Scaling** (Guo et al., ICML 2017, [arXiv:1706.04599](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.04599) **[PR]**): Fit a single scalar $T > 0$ on the validation split (`softmax(logits / T)`)—preserves accuracy while minimizing NLL and **Expected Calibration Error (ECE)**.
- **Finite-Sample Risk Control (`CIC`, Jul 2026):** Dong & Shinnou ([arXiv:2607.04430](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04430) **[PP]**) introduce *Confidence-Interval-based Calibration (`CIC`)* using Clopper–Pearson/Hoeffding upper bounds on a held-out calibration split to guarantee with probability $1-\delta$ that the accepted-action error rate stays below risk budget $\alpha$.
- **Trajectory-Level Calibration (`TC-ECE`, Sep 2026):** Lin et al. ([arXiv:2609.07395](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07395) **[PP]**) prove that single-step ECE misses error compounding across multi-turn agent trajectories (*early underconfidence, late overconfidence*); Lab evaluation should report **`TC-ECE`** alongside single-turn **ECE**, **Brier score**, and **AURC** (Geifman & El-Yaniv, NeurIPS 2017, [arXiv:1705.08500](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.08500) **[PR]**).

For `200k` params, `f32` is only `800 KB` in RAM!)*

### 2. IEEE-754 Floating-Point Determinism & `DecisionTrace::recompute`
Even in pure Rust, **three subtle sources** break bit-for-bit (`to_bits()`) cross-platform replay ([`replay.rs:4`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/replay.rs#L4)):
1. **Transcendental functions (`exp`, `tanh`, `sin`, `cos`):** Rust's `std::f64::exp` / `f32::exp` defers to the host OS `libm` (`glibc`, `musl`, `MSVC`, macOS `libSystem`), which **can differ by 1+ ULP** across OSes and architectures.
   - *Fix:* Use the pure-Rust **`libm`** crate (`rust-lang/libm`, `musl` port) for `exp`/`sin`/`cos` in the controller, or rational/piecewise approximations without `std` transcendentals.
2. **FMA Contraction & SIMD Reduction Order:** Compiler auto-vectorization or `-C target-cpu=native` can contract `a * b + c` into hardware FMA (single rounding vs two roundings) or reorder sums across `f32x4`/`f32x8` lanes.
   - *Fix:* Keep sequential `for` accumulation in `f64` (or `f32` without `-C target-feature=+fma` / fast-math) for small `<= 3M` MACs, or wrap dot products with `core::hint::black_box` / strict left-associative loops.
3. **Trace-Pinned Replay (Recommended Complementary Safeguard):** Extend `DecisionTrace` ([`decide.rs:95-112`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/gate/decide.rs#L95-L112)) to record:
   - `neural_outputs: Option<NeuralOutputs>` (raw logits/probabilities),
   - `model_blake3: Option<[u8; 32]>` (using existing [`crates/core/src/blake3.rs`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/blake3.rs)),
   - `feature_schema_version: u32`.
   This guarantees `DecisionTrace::recompute()` works **even if the weight file is absent or upgraded later**, while `replay_log()` can optionally re-run the forward pass when the matching `model_blake3` file is present.

### 3. Dimension-Agnostic Weight Format & Neutral Fallback
- **Option 1: `safetensors` (`v0.8.0`) + `__metadata__`:** Zero-copy, industry-standard (PyTorch/HuggingFace export directly to `.safetensors`), stores `shape` and `dtype` per tensor plus `__metadata__` (`arch`, `feature_schema_version`, `temperature`, `blake3`). Requires adding `safetensors = "0.8"` to `crates/core/Cargo.toml`.
- **Option 2: Minimal Versioned Binary Header + `blake3`:** `magic ("MGNC") | format_ver (u32) | json_header_len (u32) | json_header (arch, layer shapes, schema_ver, temperature) | raw f32/f16 little-endian weights | blake3_digest ([u8; 32])`. **Zero external crate dependencies** (~80 LOC parser in `crates/core`), dimension-agnostic (`50k`–`3M` loaded identically by inspecting `layer shapes`), and self-verifying via BLAKE3.

## R-62 — Out-of-the-Box Training Pipeline & Dataset Strategy (RTX 4060 8 GB)

### 1. Dataset Mapping & License Hygiene
*(Full per-dataset table in [`02_datasets_and_training_matrix.md`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/research/findings/run-5-notes/02_datasets_and_training_matrix.md), grounded in [`2026-11-17-run-3.md`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/research/findings/2026-11-17-run-3.md).)*
- **Permissive OSI Corpora for Out-of-the-Box Weights:**
  - *Turn-taking & Floor (`gate_4`, `wait_delta_t`):* **`when2speak`** (`CC-BY-4.0`, `finetune_*` train/val splits), **`ubuntu_irc` Corpus A** (`CC-BY-4.0`, native `[HH:MM]` timestamps), **`proactive_bench`** (`Apache-2.0`), **`capa`** (`MIT`/`CC-BY-4.0`, 97 train meetings), **`pitch`** (`MIT`, 35 convs — best for validation/calibration), **`MultiWOZ 2.2`** (`MIT`, dialogue acts).
  - *Memory & Epistemic (`p_memory_relevance`, `p_encode_importance`, `p_uncertainty`, `p_verify`):* **`group_mem_bench`** (`CC-BY-4.0`/`MIT`, native timestamps + `VisibleScope`), **`longmemeval`** (`MIT`, `_abs` abstention labels), **`memory_agent_bench`** (`MIT`, `Conflict_Resolution`), **`stale`** (`CC-BY-4.0`/`MIT`), **`hi_tom`** (`Apache-2.0`), **`dyntom`** (`Apache-2.0`).
- **Strictly Excluded from Default Commercial Weights:** `CC-BY-NC-4.0` (`locomo`, `opentom` [forbids training], `exploretom`, `tomi`), missing-license (`pm_bench`, `molweni`, `ubuntu_irc` B, `multilexnorm`), and `unavailable` (`pis`, `proact`, `mpbench`).

### 2. Four-Stage Training Pipeline
1. **Stage 1 — Multi-Task Supervised Pretraining:** Precompute `256d` `EmbeddingGemma 2` vectors + `18d` `x_num` features for all permissive training splits. Train with AdamW + cosine schedule and uncertainty-weighted loss (`Kendall et al., 2018`, [arXiv:1705.07115](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.07115) **[PR]**).
2. **Stage 2 — Teacher–Student Distillation:** Distill soft logits from a strong LLM judge (e.g., on `proactive_bench` / `capa` / `group_mem_bench` training splits) or an oracle deterministic harness into the `200k`-param controller using KL-divergence + temperature (`Hinton et al., 2015`).
3. **Stage 3 — DAgger & Hard-Negative Mining:** Roll out the controller inside `crates/lab` **training** scenarios (never validation/sealed), collect states where the learned policy drifts into floor collisions or missed wakes, query the oracle/teacher for corrective labels, and aggregate (`Ross et al., AISTATS 2011` **[PR]**).
4. **Stage 4 — Offline Contextual Bandit Refinement (CRM):** Optimize `gate_4` against logged bandit feedback (user uptake vs annoyance/interruption cost) using Counterfactual Risk Minimization with clipped IPS + variance regularization (`Swaminathan & Joachims, ICML 2015` **[PR]**).

### 3. Hardware Budget (RTX 4060 Laptop, 8 GB VRAM, 14 GiB RAM)
- **Key optimization:** Cache frozen `256d` embeddings as memory-mapped shards (`100k` events × `256` × `f16` = **~51 MB** on disk!).
- **Training footprint:** For `50k`–`3M` params at `batch_size = 1024`, model + AdamW + gradients + activations use **`< 800 MB` VRAM** (and `< 2 GB` even with `T=64` sequence batches for `minGRU`), running at **~0.5–12 seconds per epoch** on an RTX 4060.

### 4. Anti-Leakage Hygiene
- **Hard Exclusion:** `lab/scenarios/sealed/` is never read, mounted, or indexed by any training script.
- **Group-Aware Splitting:** Split by `conversation_id` / `meeting_id` / `scenario_id` (never by individual turn) so adjacent turns from the same dialogue never appear in both train and validation.
- **Registry Holdouts:** Official test splits of `when2speak` (`finetune_test_*`), `capa` (20 test meetings), `ubuntu_irc` (`data/test/`), `proactive_bench` (233 test events), and `longmemeval` eval items remain 100% untouched during training and distillation.

## R-64 — Unified "1 Neural Link" Cognitive Backbone (`h_t` + 5 Heads + Online Adaptation)

### 1. Recurrent Working Memory (`h_t`, `32d`–`128d` per `place`)
| Recurrent Cell | State & Gate Structure | Replay & Snapshot Fit | Cross-Turn Dynamics (`M03`, `M21`, `M22`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stateless MLP** | `h_t = None` | Zero state to serialize. | **Weak:** Relies entirely on hand-engineered `x_num` recency/habituation scalars. |
| **Gated EMA** | `z_t = sigma(W_z x_t)`, `h_t = (1-z_t)*h_{t-1} + z_t*proj(x_t)` | Trivial (`d_h` floats/place); no `h_{t-1}` matrix multiply. | Captures exponential decay/habituation (`M03`), weak on non-linear topic shifts (`M21`). |
| **`MGU`** (Zhou et al., 2016, [arXiv:1603.09420](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.09420) **[PR]**) | Single forget/update gate `f_t` coupling reset & update | Compact (`~50%` fewer params than GRU); `d_h` floats/place. | Strong sequential memory with half the MACs of standard GRU. |
| **`minGRU`** (Feng et al., 2024, [arXiv:2410.01201](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.01201) **[PP]**) | Drops `h_{t-1}` from gate computation (`z_t = sigma(Linear(x_t))`) | **Strong baseline:** `O(T)` parallel scan during PyTorch training, `O(1)` step in Rust inference (~25 LOC), `d_h` (`64`–`128` `f32`s = `256–512 B`/place) in snapshots. | Captures habituation, turn-taking rhythm, and event boundaries (`M21`), though erase & write remain coupled via `1 - z_t`. |
| **`Gated DeltaNet-2`** (Hatamizadeh, Choi, & Kautz / NVIDIA, May 2026, [arXiv:2605.22791](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22791) **[PP]**; cf. `FG²-GDN` [arXiv:2604.19021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19021) **[PP]**) | **Decoupled** channel-wise erase gate (`b_t`) and write gate (`w_t`) over a delta-rule fast-weight state | Compact fixed-size per-place state (or diagonal/low-rank `64d`–`128d` variant in pure Rust), chunkwise parallelizable in training. | **2026 state-of-the-art selective memory update:** erases stale topic/fact channels independently of writing new ones, outperforming coupled-gate recurrences on multi-key associative recall. |
| **Full `GRU`** (Cho et al., 2014) | 2 gates (`r_t`, `z_t`) depending on `h_{t-1}` | `d_h` floats/place; sequential BPTT slower to train. | Highest expressiveness per `d_h`, rarely needed over `minGRU`/`Gated DeltaNet-2` for `<=128d` control state. |

### 2. Unified 5-Head Architecture
A single shared backbone (`PLR(x_num) + W_e(emb_256d)` → `minGRU` or `Gated DeltaNet-2` (`d_h = 64..128`) → `TabM` / `Pre-LN SwiGLU` trunk) feeds 5 lightweight heads (aligned with **Gated-Memory Routing**, Sep 2026, [arXiv:2609.00237](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00237) **[PP]**, and **Memory-R1**, Aug 2025, [arXiv:2508.19828](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19828) **[PP]**):
1. **`gate_4` + `wait_delta_t`:** 4-way logits `[Silent, Note, Wait, Go]` + scalar wait duration (replaces `M01`, `M02`, `M08`; mirrors replannable timelines in [arXiv:2610.05159](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05159)).
2. **`p_uncertainty` & `p_verify`:** 2 calibrated probabilities (optionally augmented by `TabM` `k`-ensemble disagreement variance) for FoK/skepticism/abstention hints (replaces `M09`, `M14` heuristics, `M17`, `info_gap_clf`).
3. **`p_encode_importance`:** Memory Write Gate priority (replaces `M11`/`M18` heuristics).
4. **`p_memory_relevance`:** Retrieval Gate / bilinear reranker `(trunk_out, candidate_emb_256d, candidate_recency)` over `VisibleScope`-filtered candidates (replaces `M10`).
5. **Fast Online Adaptation (`M15`):**
   - **Option 1 — Frozen Backbone + Bounded Per-Place Warm-Start UCB / FiLM Contextual Bandit:** Keep backbone + recurrent cell frozen; adapt only a small per-place bias/FiLM vector (`Perez et al., 2018`, [arXiv:1709.07871](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.07871) **[PR]**) via **Warm-Start UCB Contextual Bandit** updates grounded in **MemCon** (Jul 2026, [arXiv:2607.13591](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13591) **[PP]**) and **Personalized Memory Gating** (Oct 2026, [arXiv:2610.23812](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23812) **[PP]**, specifically designed for delayed, one-sided user feedback such as ignored messages in group chats; cf. `Riquelme et al., 2018`, [arXiv:1802.09127](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.09127) **[PR]**). **Zero catastrophic forgetting** of core weights, trivial to bound (`||delta|| <= eps`) and serialize per place.
   - **Option 2 — Pure `h_t` Recurrent In-Context Adaptation:** No weight updates at runtime; recurrent state `h_t` adapts to the place's cadence and user preferences in-context. Simplest replay, zero drift risk, but forgets if `h_t` saturates over months.

### 3. Multi-Task Interference & Phased Rollout
Training heterogeneous heads (turn-taking classification, wait regression, memory ranking, uncertainty) jointly on small/medium datasets can suffer **negative transfer** when task gradients conflict (`Standley et al., ICML 2020`, [arXiv:1905.07553](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.07553) **[PR]**):
- **Mitigation during training:** Use homoscedastic uncertainty loss weighting (`Kendall et al., CVPR 2018`, [arXiv:1705.07115](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.07115) **[PR]**) and/or **PCGrad** gradient surgery (`Yu et al., NeurIPS 2020`, [arXiv:2001.06782](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.06782) **[PR]**) to project conflicting task gradients onto normal planes.
- **Recommended Phased Rollout (for Lab validation):**
  - **Phase 1:** Train & Lab-validate `gate_4` + `wait_delta_t` (using `when2speak`, `ubuntu_irc` A, `proactive_bench`, `capa`).
  - **Phase 2:** Freeze or co-train with `PCGrad` to add `p_uncertainty` & `p_verify` (`longmemeval` `_abs`, `stale`, `hi_tom`).
  - **Phase 3:** Attach `p_memory_relevance` + `p_encode_importance` (`group_mem_bench`, `memory_agent_bench`) and benchmark online adaptation (`M15`) against the baseline `learn/mod.rs` IPS delta rule.
