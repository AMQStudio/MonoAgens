# R-46 Routines that check before they speak

**Status:** partly answered

## Answer

A recurring conditional routine ("Every 30 minutes check $X$; tell me only if $Y$") is **not** cleanly expressible in `core-and-lab` using the existing single-shot `expect` record plus free-text event similarity (`expectations/spec.md`, `design.md`), and implementing it as `kind = promise` violates three existing `core-and-lab` and `bootstrap-vertical-slice` behaviors (`promise_debt` inflation, unconditional daily-budget bypass, and `max_chain_wakes` termination). Cleanly supporting recurring conditional routines without adding a sixth core book or sixth thought act (`ARCHITECTURE.md` invariant) requires extending the existing `expectations` primitive (`kind = goal` or `wait` with an optional typed `RecurrenceSpec`) to separate four concerns: (1) **drift-free recurring scheduling** (`every` UTC interval or RFC 5545 `RRULE`/cron with IANA `tz` resolved at fire time into logged UTC `due_at` timestamps for deterministic replay) with explicit **miss/catch-up policy** (`skip`, `coalesce_once`, or bounded `catch_up_window`) and **overlap policy** (`skip` default, `coalesce_one`, `allow`), keeping only at most one next instance pending after an outage rather than waking an overdue storm; (2) **two-stage active check execution**, where scheduled due time triggers an active probe of hidden state channel $X$ (optional zero-LLM `probe_cap` pre-check first, escalating to a `reason` wake only on trigger/change, or directly waking `reason` with fresh per-cycle `chain_id` anchored to `routine_id`); (3) **silent suppression without closing the schedule**, where code advances the expectation anchor $t_k \to t_{k+1}$ deterministically once the check cycle settles while allowing `reason` to end with `think`-only (no communicative `act`) whenever condition $Y$ does not hold; and (4) **strictly Gateway/Valve delivery control** outside the core mind, enforcing timezone-aware **quiet hours**, a **daily proactive notification cap**, and **predictable batched digests** (`3×/day`) over opaque action metadata (`urgency`, `routine_id`, `audience`, `is_live_reply`) using the protocol's existing `held` and `refused` receipts.

**Mathematical Rules:**

1. **Drift-free occurrence advancement & bounded outage catch-up (Core Expectation Scheduler):**
   Let $t_0$ be the anchor timestamp, $\Delta > 0$ the fixed recurrence period (or $\mathcal{N}_{\text{cal}}(\tau, \text{tz})$ the RFC 5545 calendar next-occurrence operator yielding UTC instant), $t_{\text{now}}$ the injected clock, $W_{\text{catch}} \ge 0$ the catch-up grace window, and $j_k \sim \text{PRNG}_{\text{seeded}}[0, J_{\max}]$ an optional deterministic stagger (`J_{\max} = 0` for exact timing). When an occurrence scheduled at $t_k$ is evaluated at $t_{\text{now}} \ge t_k$:
   $$\text{lateness}_k = t_{\text{now}} - t_k$$
   $$t_{k+1}^{\text{raw}} = \begin{cases} t_0 + \left(\left\lfloor \dfrac{t_{\text{now}} - t_0}{\Delta} \right\rfloor + 1\right)\Delta, & \text{fixed interval }\Delta \\[8pt] \min \{ \tau > t_{\text{now}} : \tau \in \mathcal{N}_{\text{cal}}(\text{spec}, \text{tz}) \}, & \text{calendar rule (log resolved UTC }\tau\text{)} \end{cases}$$
   $$t_{k+1} = t_{k+1}^{\text{raw}} + j_{k+1}$$
   The missed-occurrence action $a_{\text{miss}}(t_k, t_{\text{now}})$ with previous run state $s_{\text{prev}} \in \{\text{idle}, \text{active}\}$ is:
   $$a_{\text{miss}} = \begin{cases} \text{defer\_or\_skip}_{\text{overlap}} & \text{if } s_{\text{prev}} = \text{active} \text{ (default } \text{skip}\text{; } \text{coalesce\_one} \text{ queues } 1\text{)} \\ \text{skip\_to}(t_{k+1}) & \text{if } \text{policy} = \text{skip} \lor \text{lateness}_k > W_{\text{catch}} \\ \text{fire\_once\_and\_advance}(t_{k+1}) & \text{if } \text{policy} = \text{coalesce\_once} \land \text{lateness}_k \le W_{\text{catch}} \\ \text{replay}(\min(N_{\text{missed}}, N_{\text{catch,max}})) & \text{if } \text{policy} = \text{catch\_up\_window} \land \text{lateness}_k \le W_{\text{catch}} \end{cases}$$
   Every cycle allocates a fresh chain counter bounded by `max_chain_wakes` and carries only opaque `routine_id` provenance (never inflating `promise_debt` and never inheriting `promise` budget bypass).

2. **Silent Check vs Conditional Notification (Core Lifecycle Separation):**
   For cycle outcome $u_k \in \{\text{silent}(\neg Y), \text{notify}(Y), \text{failed}\}$ at check completion:
   $$\text{schedule\_alive}_{k+1} = \mathbb{I}[\text{not explicitly resolved/cancelled} \land (E_{\text{expiry}} = \varnothing \lor t_{k+1} \le E_{\text{expiry}})]$$
   $$\text{emit\_act}_{\text{notify}} = \mathbb{I}[u_k = \text{notify}(Y)]$$
   Thus $u_k = \text{silent}(\neg Y)$ suppresses any communicative `act` while still preserving $\text{schedule\_alive}_{k+1} = 1$ at $t_{k+1}$.

3. **Valve Notification Gate & Digest Routing (Gateway Action Door — Never Core):**
   For an outbound communicative action $a$ targeting `audience` $g$ with recipient-local wall-clock time-of-day $\tau_{\text{loc}}(t_{\text{now}}, \text{tz}_g) \in [0, 24\text{h})$ and local date $d_{\text{loc}}$, urgency score $u(a) \in [0, 1]$, live conversational reply flag $r_{\text{live}}(a) \in \{0, 1\}$, and daily proactive delivery counter $c_g(d_{\text{loc}})$:
   $$\text{in\_quiet}(\tau) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{I}[\tau \in [Q_{\text{start}}, Q_{\text{end}})], & Q_{\text{start}} < Q_{\text{end}} \\ \mathbb{I}[\tau \ge Q_{\text{start}} \lor \tau < Q_{\text{end}}], & Q_{\text{start}} > Q_{\text{end}} \\ 0, & Q_{\text{start}} = Q_{\text{end}} \end{cases}$$
   $$\text{route}(a, t_{\text{now}}) = \begin{cases} \text{deliver\_now}, & r_{\text{live}}(a) = 1 \;\lor\; \big((\neg\text{in\_quiet} \lor u(a) \ge U_{\text{quiet}}) \land (c_g < C_{\text{daily}} \lor u(a) \ge U_{\text{cap}}) \land u(a) \ge U_{\text{imm}}\big) \\ \text{refuse(daily\_cap)}, & c_g \ge C_{\text{daily}} \land u(a) < U_{\text{cap}} \land B_{\text{digest}} \text{ disabled or full} \\ \text{hold\_digest}(t_{\text{next\_slot}}), & \text{otherwise (deduplicate by } \text{routine\_id} \text{ until } t_{\text{next\_slot}} \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{digest}}\text{)} \end{cases}$$
   When $\text{route}(a, t_{\text{now}}) = \text{hold\_digest}(t_{\text{next\_slot}})$, Valve issues receipt `held` (not `done` and not silent drop) with the release window so Core accurately knows delivery has not yet occurred; Valve flushes the digest buffer at fixed local slots $\mathcal{S}_{\text{digest}}$ (e.g., $K_{\text{digest}} = 3$ active-hour slots/day).

## Neutral value

- **Core recurrence neutral value:** `recurrence = None` (single-shot expectation, identical to current `core-and-lab` behavior), with $J_{\max} = 0$, $\text{overlap\_policy} = \text{skip}$, and $W_{\text{catch}} = 0$ (no catch-up).
- **Valve notification policy neutral value:** $Q_{\text{start}} = Q_{\text{end}}$ (quiet hours disabled), $C_{\text{daily}} = +\infty$ (uncapped), and $U_{\text{imm}} = 0.0$ (immediate pass-through, digest batching disabled). Every communicative `act` passes directly to the Body unless blocked by hard safety/permission rules, and the cognitive loop still runs unchanged.

## Claim

1. **Routine correctness & anti-spam claim:** Without typed recurrence (`anchor` + `miss_policy` + silent check separation + non-`promise` accounting), recurring conditional routines either suffer phase drift and permanent silent loss on budget refusal/restart, inflate `promise_debt` and illegally bypass `valve`'s daily token cap, burst backlogged wake storms across outages (`catch-up` storm), or spam users with unconditioned pings on every check (`PM-Bench arXiv:2607.12385`: fixed 30m auto-heartbeat produced `489` false positives across 8 runs vs `134` for a structured todo ledger and `178` for selective monitoring; `PIS arXiv:2609.01272`: moving typed lifecycle state tracking into deterministic code lifted DeepSeek-Chat Set-F1 to `82.9%` vs `46.5%–58.3%` under retrospective memory stacks and Gemma-E2B from `<= 6.6%` to `66.2%`).
2. **Valve interruption-hygiene claim:** Without deterministic Valve quiet hours, daily caps, and predictable batched digests (`3×/day`), routine alerts fragment user attention at inopportune moments, increase negative affect (`Pielot et al. 2014`), disrupt ongoing tasks even when content is useful (`Mehrotra et al. 2016`), and degrade focus compared to predictable 3x/day batching (`Fitz et al. 2019`), whereas turning alerts off altogether induces FoMO/anxiety (`Fitz et al. 2019`). Putting policy solely in Valve guarantees Core remains content-blind and keeps a 24/7 sentinel gate (`ARCHITECTURE.md`; `A-04`).

## Established / inferred / open

### Architecture & `core-and-lab` / `bootstrap-vertical-slice` Issues Identified
- **[Gap in `core-and-lab/specs/expectations/spec.md` L12–13 & `design.md` L262–263]:** `expect(kind, about_ids, condition, due, expiry)` represents only single-shot `due`/`expiry`; it lacks recurrence specification (`interval` / `rrule` / `tz` / `anchor`), `miss_policy`, `overlap_policy`, and optional deterministic probe bindings.
- **[Gap in `core-and-lab/specs/expectations/spec.md` L111–113]:** Free-text expectation conditions only match passive incoming events by vector cosine similarity (`wake_gate_lo`); they do not trigger active periodic polling of hidden state channels (`check X`).
- **[Contradiction / Misuse hazard with `promise` in `expectations/spec.md` L103–104 & `bootstrap-vertical-slice/specs/valve/spec.md` L46–47, L58–59]:** Treating a recurring monitor as `kind = promise` pollutes `promise_debt` in the gate, grants unconditional exemption from the owner's `daily_token_budget` (`"except for wakes caused by a fired promise"`), and collides with `max_chain_wakes` if consecutive checks share one chain.
- **[Defect in `core-and-lab/specs/expectations/spec.md` L28–30]:** Waking all overdue expectations at startup creates an unbounded catch-up storm for recurring checks after extended downtime rather than consulting per-routine `miss_policy` and `W_catch`.

### What is Established vs Inferred vs Open
- **Established:**
  - Human cognitive science distinguishes **time-based** (monitoring/clock checking) vs **event-based** (cue-driven) vs **habitual** (recurring, repetition-error-prone) prospective memory (`Einstein & McDaniel 1990 [PR]`; `Einstein et al. 1998 [PR]`; `Einstein & McDaniel 2005 [PR]`).
  - LLM agents fail heavily at cross-day updates, missed triggers, and monitoring false positives when relying on passive prompts or fixed polling (`PM-Bench arXiv:2607.12385 [PP]`: 30m auto-heartbeat `489` FP vs `134` todo ledger), whereas separating **deterministic schema/lifecycle state management in code** + **active hidden-channel queries** from scoped semantic judgment lifts performance dramatically (`PIS arXiv:2609.01272 [PP]`: DeepSeek-Chat `82.9%` Set-F1, Gemma-E2B `66.2%` vs `<=6.6%`; `Pro-Memory arXiv:2611.05731 [PP]`: explicit $\omega_i \in \{\text{ONCE}, \text{RECURRING}\}$ typing + selective enactment reaches `76.00%` PM-Bench Set-F1 on Claude-Sonnet 4.5).
  - Production agent & distributed schedulers universally put recurrence timing outside the model/task, pin IANA timezones, separate execution from overlap (`Temporal` `OverlapPolicy`, `Kubernetes` `concurrencyPolicy`), bound outage catch-up (`Temporal` `CatchupWindow`, `Kubernetes` `startingDeadlineSeconds` & `100`-miss cutoff, `OpenClaw` startup rescheduling, `Hermes` `catch_up` & `grace_minutes`), and support silent non-delivery on empty/`HEARTBEAT_OK` checks (`OpenClaw`, `Hermes`).
  - Calendar recurrence and DST anomaly rules (skip non-existent spring-forward time / disambiguate fall-back folds) and timezone-annotated timestamps are standardized in `RFC 5545` §3.3.5 and `RFC 9557` (`IXDTF`) `[IND]`.
  - Real-time unbatched smartphone notifications trigger high interruption load (`Pielot et al. 2014 [PR]`: mean `65.3` notifications/day in body / `63.5` in abstract across 33 apps, median view within `3.5–27.7 min`, linked to negative emotions alongside connectedness) and even useful notifications cause task disruption depending on sender relationship and task state (`Mehrotra et al. 2016 CHI [PR]`), while user-approved contextual filters can be mined accurately (`Mehrotra et al. 2016 UbiComp [PR]`: `56.98%` rule acceptance, `100%` precision over user-approved rules). Predictable **`3×/day` batching** improves attention, focus, control, and mood over both hourly batching and no-notifications (`Fitz et al. 2019 [PR]`). *(Note: exact regression effect-size betas from Fitz et al. 2019 could not be directly verified beyond the 4-arm N=237 design and directional conclusions due to publisher paywall.)*
- **Inferred (MonoAgens design synthesis):**
  - Mapping recurring checks into `expectations` (`RecurrenceSpec` without `promise` debt/bypass) plus emitting logged UTC `next_due_utc` preserves `ARCHITECTURE.md`'s fixed-primitive, replayable, content-blind core invariants while preventing `LangGraph`/UTC-only DST bugs (`LangGraph` evaluates cron purely in UTC).
  - Returning a protocol `held` receipt when Valve defers an alert into quiet hours / digest batching keeps Core's expectation/prediction bookkeeping consistent without leaking Core-side knowledge of quiet hours or owners.
  - Pre-filtering obvious numeric/non-empty changes via deterministic `probe_cap` before waking `reason` extends `PIS`'s code-vs-LLM split to cut token cost on frequent 30-minute checks.
- **Open (not settled by literature — requires Lab/user calibration):**
  - Exact optimal **daily agent-initiated cap** $C_{\text{daily}}$ (distinct from all-phone total `~63.5/day`), urgency cutoffs ($U_{\text{imm}}, U_{\text{quiet}}, U_{\text{cap}}$), and task-specific $W_{\text{catch}}$ defaults.
  - False-negative vs cost curve when evaluating ambiguous natural-language condition $Y$ with small local models vs frontier models on every $30\text{m}$ wake inside MonoAgens's opaque-ID harness.
  - Whether a Valve-flushed digest batch should concatenate raw opaque notification summaries deterministically or re-admit a single bounded consolidation `reason` wake when $>N$ items accumulate from one routine.

# R-47 Delivery that reads as human

**Status:** partly answered

## Answer

**None** of typing delay, message/bubble splitting, or chat typing indicators belongs in **MonoAgens Core** or the generic **Core↔Gateway Protocol**: `ARCHITECTURE.md` mandates that the Core sees only opaque IDs, numbers, and descriptions, never owns platform- or body-specific mechanics, and treats speaking as a capability like any other—typing dots and split chat bubbles are **strictly chat-only** mechanics that make no sense for sensors, batch emails, game agents, or physical robots. **All** message splitting (platform length limits and optional readability chunking), typing-dot keepalives, and conversational formatting belong strictly inside the **Chat Body (Adapter)**, while **honest, body-generic execution-state signaling** during genuine model or tool latency belongs on **Waist 2 (`gateway ↔ plugins` lifecycle events)**—so a Chat Body can pulse a native typing indicator (after $1.0\text{ s}$) or update an in-place status note (after $10.0\text{ s}$), and a robot or voice body can translate that same lifecycle signal into an LED/posture cue or audio earcon without altering Core state. Empirically, **artificial post-completion response delays and fake typing hesitation are counterproductive and legally hazardous as of November 2026**: `Gnewuch et al. (2022)` ($N=202$) proved delayed responses ($M=2.3\text{ s}$) help novices only while **reducing** social presence and usage intentions for experienced users, and found **no significant difference between static and dynamic delays**; `Zhou & Hu (2025)` found hesitation-only typing hurt naturalness and response-time ratings while self-editing lowered response-time scores; and **EU AI Act (2024/1689) Article 50(1)** (in force since **2 August 2026**) requires clear AI interaction transparency, prohibiting deceptive human impersonation via fabricated typing pauses once generation is complete. Finally, when a Chat Body splits **1 Core communicative `act`** into $M \ge 1$ platform bubbles, it MUST register **all $M$ outbound platform message IDs** in its correlation index (`R-09` self-caused echo suppression and reply-to-later-chunk linking) and return **exactly one final `receipt`** (`done`, partial/barge-in status, or failure) after all chunks finish or abort.

**Mathematical Rules (Executed Strictly in Gateway Waist 2 & Chat Body — Zero Core Footprint):**

1. **Honest In-Flight Waist 2 Status Signaling (Body-Generic Timing Rule):**
   Let $t_{\text{now}}$ be wall time at Gateway/Body, $t_{\text{start}}$ the start of genuine active work (model inference or an in-flight `act(capability)`), $e(t) = \mathbb{I}[\text{turn or action genuinely active at } t] \in \{0, 1\}$, and $t_{\text{elap}} = t_{\text{now}} - t_{\text{start}}$:
   $$\text{working\_cue}(t_{\text{now}}) = \mathbb{I}\big[e(t_{\text{now}}) = 1 \;\land\; t_{\text{elap}} \ge T_{\text{cue,start}}\big] \quad \text{(Body pulses native indicator every } T_{\text{pulse}}\text{)}$$
   $$\text{progress\_note}(t_{\text{now}}) = \mathbb{I}\big[e(t_{\text{now}}) = 1 \;\land\; t_{\text{elap}} \ge T_{\text{prog,start}} \;\land\; (t_{\text{now}} - t_{\text{last\_note}}) \ge T_{\text{prog,int}}\big]$$
   Chat Body translates $\text{working\_cue}$ into platform `typing`/`chatAction` and $\text{progress\_note}$ into a concise non-intrusive status message (edited/cleared on delivery); embodied Bodies translate them into non-textual physical/audio feedback (or ignore on async/batch bodies).

2. **Non-Deceptive Delivery & Atomic Split-Bubble Contract (Chat Body Only):**
   Once Core emits communicative action $a$ with finalized payload text $s$, the Chat Body segments $s$ into chunks $(c_1, \dots, c_M)$ of lengths $|c_m| \le L_{\text{cap}}$ where $L_{\text{cap}} = \min(L_{\text{plat}}, L_{\text{soft}})$ (with $L_{\text{soft}} = +\infty$ by default so splitting only occurs for hard platform character limits unless an explicit opt-in UX test sets $L_{\text{soft}}$):
   $$d_1 = d_{\text{art}} = 0\text{ ms (neutral/default: no artificial post-completion delay)}$$
   $$d_m = \begin{cases} 0\text{ ms}, & d_{\text{gap}} = 0 \text{ (default)} \\ \min\big(D_{\text{gap,max}},\; \alpha_{\text{gap}} \cdot |c_{m-1}|\big), & \text{if user-opted reading pacing } (m \ge 2) \end{cases}$$
   If an inbound user barge-in event arrives on the same `place` while chunk $m \in \{2,\dots,M\}$ is queued: abort chunks $\{m,\dots,M\}$, return **one** Core `receipt` for action $a$ carrying the sent chunk count $(m-1)/M$ and registered platform IDs $\{\text{pid}_1, \dots, \text{pid}_{m-1}\}$ so self-echo suppression (`R-09`) and `response`/`effect` expectations stay exact.

## Neutral value

- **Core:** Literally zero code or state in Core (`0` hooks, `0` parameters, `0` behavioral change).
- **Gateway / Body:** $T_{\text{cue,start}} = +\infty$, $T_{\text{prog,start}} = +\infty$, $d_{\text{art}} = 0\text{ ms}$, $d_{\text{gap}} = 0\text{ ms}$, $L_{\text{soft}} = +\infty$ (only hard platform $L_{\text{plat}}$ chunking when required by API limits, registering all chunk IDs in the Body correlation map). The Core loop and Gateway protocol run identically whether indicators/chunking are enabled or disabled.

## Claim

1. **Body isolation claim:** Keeping all typing delays, bubble splitting, and platform indicators strictly in the Chat Body (with zero Core/Protocol fields) guarantees that non-chat bodies (sensors, webhooks, game agents, physical robots) never inherit meaningless chat artifacts (`ARCHITECTURE.md` body-genericity invariant), while keeping Core deterministic (`d_art = 0` inside Core, no Core timing pollution across adapters).
2. **Delivery quality & compliance claim:** Replacing fabricated typing delays ($d_{\text{art}} > 0$) with honest in-flight indicators ($T_{\text{cue,start}} = 1\text{ s}$, $T_{\text{prog,start}} = 10\text{ s}$) eliminates the negative social-presence and usage-intention penalty suffered by experienced users (`Gnewuch et al. 2022`), avoids the naturalness/responsiveness penalty of simulated hesitation (`Zhou & Hu 2025`), reduces user anxiety during genuine long tool waits (`Myers 1985`; `Nah 2004`), and stays compliant with **EU AI Act Art. 50(1)** transparency requirements (applied 2 August 2026).
3. **Protocol correctness claim:** Without tracking all $M$ chunk platform IDs inside the Chat Body and emitting a single aggregated `receipt` after final chunk delivery/barge-in, splitting one Core `act` breaks `R-09` self-caused event filtering, breaks reply-to-chunk grounding (`Hermes` `test_cron_reply_to_later_chunk.py`), and prematurely fires or falsifies `response` predictions.

## How the Lab would test it

Because **subjective user preference for human-paced multi-bubble delivery cannot be measured by LLM-only synthetic simulations alone**, testing splits into (A) **Lab mechanical correctness harnesses** and (B) **human-subject A/B UX evaluation**:

1. **Lab Harness Tests (Deterministic Discrete-Event Sim & Fake Chat Body):**
   - **Test 1 (Split-Bubble & `R-09` Echo / Barge-In Integrity):** Configure fake Chat Body with `L_plat = 200` and a `500`-char `act(say)` producing `M = 3` outbound messages (`pid_1, pid_2, pid_3`). **Ablation:** naively return `receipt(done)` on `pid_1` and track only `pid_1` vs proposed multi-chunk correlation table + single `done` after `pid_3`. Inject: (i) delayed platform echo of `pid_3`, (ii) user reply quoting `pid_2`, and (iii) user barge-in between `pid_1` and `pid_2`. **Verify:** zero self-echo misclassifications (`R-09` precision = `1.0`), accurate aborted count `1/3` in `receipt` on barge-in, and zero false `response`-prediction timeouts.
   - **Test 2 (Honest Lifecycle Cue Gating):** Replay mock tool runs taking `0.3 s`, `3.0 s`, and `18.0 s`. **Verify:** `0` working cues emitted for `0.3 s` turns (`< 1.0 s`), `working_cue` emitted without text spam for `3.0 s` runs, `1` concise `progress_note` emitted past `10.0 s`, and **`0 ms` extra stalling** added after the simulated LLM finishes generating.
2. **Human-Subject Experiment (Proposed Study to Settle Multi-Bubble Chunking & Delay Moderation):**
   - **Design:** $2 \times 3$ mixed factorial study ($N \ge 120$, adequately powered unlike `Zhou & Hu 2025`'s $N=11$ pilot), crossing **prior AI familiarity** (novice vs regular experienced users, `Gnewuch et al. 2022` covariate) $\times$ **delivery format** (`single structured markdown block at 0 ms` vs `semantic multi-bubble split with 0 ms gap` vs `semantic multi-bubble split with bounded reading pause + honest step indicator`), with **explicit AI disclosure** per EU AI Act Art. 50.
   - **Measures:** comprehension accuracy on multi-item answers, reading time, perceived cognitive load (NASA-TLX), naturalness, irritation/impatience, screen-reader/accessibility burden, and follow-up reply quality. This directly resolves the open empirical question on soft multi-bubble splitting.

## Established / inferred / open

### Architecture & `core-and-lab` / `bootstrap-vertical-slice` Boundary Check
- **`ARCHITECTURE.md` & `core-and-lab/specs/protocol/spec.md` alignment:** Waist 2 (`gateway ↔ plugins`) already names `lifecycle events` (`ARCHITECTURE.md` L207–208) and `Every action ends in exactly one receipt` (`ARCHITECTURE.md` L198). Implementing split-chunk correlation (`pid_1..pid_M` → one `action_id`) and body-side status rendering preserves the Core protocol untouched.
- **Note on `bootstrap-vertical-slice`:** No multi-chunk correlation index or barge-in truncation metadata contract is currently specified in `gateway/spec.md`; the Chat Body adapter specification in future changes must explicitly mandate multi-chunk ID tracking (`Hermes` `#26714` precedent) and single aggregated receipts so `R-09` and `response` expectations remain sound.

### What is Established vs Inferred vs Open
- **Established:**
  - **Response-time delays have opposing effects by user expertise:** In `Gnewuch et al. (2022)` `[PR]` ($N=202$, `200–400 ms` INST vs `M=2.3 s` DLY), delays increased social presence and intention to use for novices **but decreased both for experienced chatbot users**; additionally, **no difference was found between static and dynamic delays**. `Schanke et al. (2021)` `[PR]` showed dynamic `70 wpm` delays lowered likability (as cited in `Gnewuch et al. 2022`) even when broader humanizing cues increased conversion, offer elasticity, and disclosure. Earlier studies (`Gnewuch et al. 2018` ECIS / Pre-ICIS `[PR]`; `Holtgraves et al. 2007` `[PR]` `50 ms/char`) found benefits mostly under novice/first-time expectations. *(Note: full PDFs of Gnewuch et al. 2018 ECIS/Pre-ICIS are gated behind AISeL login; summary and exact findings were corroborated via Gnewuch et al. 2022 open-access full text and AISeL metadata.)*
  - **Simulated typing hesitation and self-editing remain unvalidated and risky:** `Zhou & Hu (2025)` `[PP]` (`arXiv:2510.08912v1`, pilot $N=11$ analyzed out of $20$ recruited) found hesitation-only behavior lowered naturalness (`2.272` vs `2.909`) and human-likeness (`1.727` vs `2.455`) and harmed response-time satisfaction (`3.455` vs `4.550` baseline), while hesitation+self-editing lowered response-time rating (`3.818` vs `4.550`) with **no statistically significant** gains on subjective scales.
  - **Latency thresholds and genuine progress notes help:** `0.1 s` instantaneous, `1.0 s` continuous thought limit, and `10 s` focus-retention limit (`Card et al. 1991` `[PR]`; `Nielsen 1993` `[IND]`), `~2 s` web tolerable waiting time prolonged by feedback (`Nah 2004` `[PR]`), and user preference/reduced anxiety with percent-done progress indicators (`Myers 1985` `[PR]`). `Zhang et al. (2024)` `[PR]` ($N=194$) showed verbal delay justifications boost transparency/trust in fixed-wait contexts, **not** during dynamic delays.
  - **Deception & transparency stakes are high and legally binding in the EU:** `Luo et al. (2019)` `[PR]` demonstrated large customer-behavior shifts upon bot disclosure (`79.7%` drop in purchases once disclosed up front), illustrating why deceptive mimicking undermines informed interaction; **Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (EU AI Act) Article 50(1)** (`[IND]`, in force since **2 August 2026** per Article 113) requires direct-interaction AI systems to inform natural persons that they are interacting with an AI unless obvious.
- **Inferred (MonoAgens design synthesis):**
  - Strictly placing `0 ms` post-completion pacing, native typing pulse forwarding from Waist 2 `lifecycle events`, platform length chunking, and multi-chunk ID correlation inside the Chat Body satisfies both `ARCHITECTURE.md` ("an adapter never thinks" — mechanical pacing/chunking upon finalized Core `act` without altering semantics or adding cognition) and empirical/legal safeguards.
- **Open / Not settled by the literature:**
  - **Single clean block vs multiple sequential chat bubbles (outside hard platform character limits):** No verified peer-reviewed controlled experiment establishes clear superiority of splitting an already-generated response into timed multiple chat bubbles vs formatted single-block delivery across task types (casual chat vs technical support vs accessibility/screen readers).
  - Exact optimal frequency and wording granularity of Waist 2 intermediate tool-execution progress notes ($T_{\text{prog,start}}, T_{\text{prog,int}}$) during long multi-step agent tool calls (`> 10 s` waits).
