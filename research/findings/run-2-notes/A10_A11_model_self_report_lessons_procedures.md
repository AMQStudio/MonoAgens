# A-10 What reasoning must supply

**Status:** partly answered

## Answer

Reasoning SHALL NOT be treated as a trustworthy narrator of its own probabilities, evidence graph, utility payoffs, or taxonomy labels: `core-and-lab` MUST split self-report into **schema-enforced, bounded discrete judgments** where the model adds information (epistemic uncertainty, timing relevance, asymmetric response stakes, short affirmative expectation) and **deterministic core computation / verification** wherever graph provenance, agency, vector similarity, or commitment links already exist in the log. Concretely: (1) all optional fields on thought acts (`importance`, `basis`, `value`, `cost`, `expected`, `domain_id`) MUST be encoded as **required nullable** JSON Schema fields (`"anyOf": [schema, {"type": "null"}]`, `"additionalProperties": false`) rather than omitted optional keys, because strict tool-calling modes reject non-required schemas across `11%–94%` of real schemas (`Geng et al., 2025`); (2) per-answer report-control fields (`confidence`, `value`, `cost`) MUST live on the communicative `act` itself (or explicitly reference an `act_index`) rather than sitting solely on `think`, preventing ambiguity when one wake combines a belief `think` with one or more `act`s; (3) `confidence` is elicited as a coupled **integer `0–20`** plus a short rationale string (`confidence_reason`) to maximize metacognitive sensitivity and stop constant-value collapse, while `motivation` (`1–5`) is requested **only on unallocated wakes** alongside brief positive/negative timing heuristics; (4) `basis` and `used_ids[]` are **mechanically verified against the log and wake `ContextPlan`** (`provenance` + `citation lock`), blocking ungrounded `observed` / `self` claims and fabricated IDs; and (5) unconstrained float `value`/`cost`, uncalibrated `importance`, and open-text `domain` invention are replaced by bounded ordinal/rubric scales or deterministic core computation (`max(i_commit, i_surprise)` and nearest of $K$ sleep centroids), with explicit neutral fallbacks when any field is omitted (`null`).

### Mathematical Formulation & Elicitation Specification

#### 10.0 Schema Encoding Rule (All 5 Thought Acts)
Under strict JSON Schema tool decoding (`OpenAI Structured Outputs [IND]`; `Geng et al., 2025 [PP]` `arXiv:2501.10868`), schemas using optional omitted keys are rejected because `strict: true` mandates `"additionalProperties": false` and listing every key in `"required"`. In `JSONSchemaBench`, naive open-source decoding achieves only `0.90` compliance on `GlaiveAI`, `0.65` on `GitHub Easy`, `0.38` on `GitHub Medium`, and `0.13` on `GitHub Hard`; `OpenAI strict` reaches `1.00` compliance on `GlaiveAI` and `Hard` when supported, **only** if optional keys are encoded as **required nullable fields**. Moreover, schema validity does not imply value accuracy: in `SOB` (`Singh et al., 2026 [PP]` `arXiv:2604.25359`), 21 models hit `0.965–0.993` JSON pass rate (`GPT-5.4` `0.993`), yet exact leaf-value match reached only `0.798` (`Perfect = 0.469`, max text value accuracy `83.0%`). Therefore:
- Every "optional" field in Decision 9 (`importance`, `basis`, `told_by_actor_id`, `stakes_tier` / `value` & `cost`, `expected`, `domain_id`, `confidence_reason`) MUST be listed in `"required"` with `"anyOf": [<typed_schema>, {"type": "null"}]`.
- Malformed or missing required fields trigger at most $N_{\text{reask}} = 1$ retry with explicit schema-error feedback before applying deterministic neutral fallbacks (`model-providers/spec.md`).

#### 10.1 `confidence` ($c \in [0, 1]$)
- **Empirical Evidence:**
  - `Tian et al. (2023) [PR]` (`arXiv:2305.14975`): On RLHF models, 1-step verbalized top-1 confidence cuts ECE by `>50%` vs sequence label logprobs on `TriviaQA` (`gpt-3.5-turbo`: ECE `0.140 -> 0.068`, AUC `0.869 -> 0.879`; `gpt-4`: ECE `0.078 -> 0.024`, AUC `0.937`), though harder datasets remain miscalibrated (`SciQ` GPT-4 ECE `0.201`, `TruthfulQA` ECE `0.350`, AUC `0.622`). Crucially, separate **2-step CoT confidence (`Verb. 2S CoT`) worsened calibration** vs 1-step coupled confidence on `gpt-3.5-turbo` (`SciQ` ECE `0.234 -> 0.323`; `TruthfulQA` `0.389 -> 0.431`).
  - `Xiong et al. (2024) [PR]` (`arXiv:2306.13063`): Across 8 datasets, vanilla single-pass verbalized confidence shows high avg ECE (`52.0%` GPT-3, `46.1%` Vicuna, `43.6%` LLaMA-2, `37.7%` GPT-3.5, `18.0%` GPT-4) and weak avg AUROC (`51.3%` to `62.7%`), clustering at human-like multiples of 5 in `80%–100%`.
  - `Dai & Wang (2026) [PP]` (`arXiv:2603.09309v5`): Under `[0, 100]`, Top-3 round numbers consume **`78.2%–92.1%`** of all outputs across 6 models (`Gemini 3.1 Pro` outputs `100` on `68.4%` of queries with ~79% conditional accuracy; `Qwen3-30B` Top-3 = `90.3%`, only `15` unique values, Shannon entropy `1.01` bits). Sweeping granularities `[0,5], [0,10], [0,20], [0,50], [0,100]`, **`[0, 20]` achieves the highest metacognitive sensitivity ($meta\text{-}d'$) and efficiency ($M_{\text{ratio}}$) for every tested model** (`GPT-5.2`: $M_{\text{ratio}} = 0.95$ vs $0.92$ at `[0,100]`, $meta\text{-}d' = 1.92$ vs $1.84$; `Gemini 3.1 Pro`: $0.79$ vs $0.74$; `Llama-3-8B-Instruct`: $0.79$ vs $0.71$; `[0,5]` is worst). Standard integer scales yield `0.0%–0.8%` out-of-range violations (`[0, 20]`: `0.0%–0.8%`) vs `8.6%–21.2%` on irregular scales like `[3, 38]` (Table 21).
  - `Cao et al. (2026) [PP]` (`arXiv:2605.14186`): Separating pre-solve feeling-of-knowing (`FOK`) and post-solve judgment-of-learning (`JOL`) with explicit rationale fields (`FOK_reason`, `JOL_reason` — which prevent calibration collapse to a constant default such as `0.8`) lifts pooled reasoning accuracy `+8.6 pp` (`48.3% -> 56.9%` on `Sonnet-4.6`). However, across 9 frontier LLMs (`AUROC >= 0.60, ECE <= 0.15` bar), **only `Sonnet-4.6` passes all 6 raw & joint rows**, `Opus-4.6` sits at threshold, `6/9` are **discriminative but raw-miscalibrated** (e.g., `GPT-OSS`: `JOL AUROC = 0.87`, raw `JOL ECE = 0.41`, tightened by isotonic SVM to `ECE = 0.06`), and `Gemma-4` fails FOK discrimination entirely. Removing the calibrated SVM boundary for a hand-crafted heuristic drops accuracy from `47.0%` to `42.0%`.
  - `Gupta (2026) [PP]` (`arXiv:2610.31657`): At an asymmetric break-even threshold $p \ge 0.80$, raw verbalized scores cannot be used directly as probabilities; even after isotonic calibration, **7 of 8 prompted LLM settings defer 100% of items (0/1,000 routed)**, and one `GPT-4o-mini` setting collapses to a single confidence value (`AUROC = 0.500`).
- **Formula & Elicitation:** Elicit an integer $c_{\text{raw}} \in \{0, 1, \dots, S_{\text{conf}}\}$ with $S_{\text{conf}} = 20$ alongside a 1-sentence justification `confidence_reason` (`<= 120` chars) coupled directly on the communicative `act` (for `P_rc`) and on `think` (for unallocated turns & belief confidence):
  $$c_{\text{norm}} = \mathrm{clamp}\!\left(\frac{c_{\text{raw}}}{S_{\text{conf}}},\; 0,\; 1\right), \qquad \hat{c} = f_{\text{cal}, m}\!\left(c_{\text{norm}}\right)$$
  where $c_{\text{raw}}$ is the discrete LLM self-rating on $\{0,\dots,20\}$, $S_{\text{conf}} = 20$ is the scale ceiling, $c_{\text{norm}} \in [0, 1]$ is the normalized raw confidence, and $f_{\text{cal}, m}: [0, 1] \to [0, 1]$ is an optional monotone per-provider calibration curve (identity $f_{\text{cal}}(x)=x$ at neutral / before calibration data; Platt/isotonic fitted offline in Lab once $\ge 50$ resolved outcomes exist).
- **Deterministic Core Monitoring & Fallbacks:** Core detects constant-value collapse automatically whenever rolling window std over the last $W_{\text{cal}} = 30$ communicative/unallocated scores satisfies $\sigma(c_{\text{norm}}) < \epsilon_{\text{collapse}}$ ($0.05$). If omitted (`null`) on an unallocated turn, apply fail-closed $c_{\text{fallback, unalloc}} = 0.0$ (withhold unallocated `act`); if omitted on an answer subject to `P_rc`, trigger the standard below-threshold `recall` -> coarsen -> withhold loop (`c < P_rc`).

#### 10.2 `motivation` ($m \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$)
- **Empirical Evidence:** `Liu et al. (2025) [PR]` (`doi:10.1145/3706598.3713760`, `arXiv:2501.00383v2`, *Inner Thoughts*) elicits 1–5 proactive participation motivation in Slack-style group chat and achieves `82%` human preference over a reactive baseline (`U = 577.0, p = 2.4e-6` turn appropriateness; `U = 636.0, p = 1.6e-5` coherence). Crucially, Sec. 5.4 documents that **omitting negative heuristics causes LLMs to systematically overrate motivation**, so the prompt must require listing top positive and top negative factors before rating. Furthermore, Liu et al. compute a continuous score via G-Eval **first-token logprob weighting** $\sum_{i=1}^5 p(s_i) s_i \cdot \lambda^{t-\tau_p}$ ($\lambda=1.02$) rather than trusting a plain JSON integer—an option unavailable on standard OpenAI-compatible function/tool-calling endpoints without token logprobs over schema fields, and `Liu et al.` never evaluated standalone calibration of the 1–5 score. (And in MonoAgens, temporal pressure $\lambda^{t-\tau_p}$ belongs to deterministic core `patience` / `salience`, not inside the LLM prompt.)
- **Formula & Elicitation:** Required nullable `motivation: 1..5 | null` + short `motivation_balance` (one brief reason to speak + one reason to stay silent) **only when `turn_allocated = false`**. On allocated wakes (`turn_allocated = true`), `motivation` is nullable and set to `null` (saves schema compliance errors and tokens).
- **Deterministic Core Check & Fallback:** Gate condition remains $m \ge \text{speaking\_bar}$ (`reasoning/spec.md` L126). If `motivation` is omitted (`null`) on an unallocated wake when $\text{speaking\_bar} > 1$, core fails closed ($m_{\text{fallback}} = 1 \implies$ withhold communicative `act`) rather than defaulting to `5`. Neutral mechanism value is $\text{speaking\_bar} = 1$ (every valid $m \in \{1..5\}$ passes). *Body-specificity note:* Unallocated-turn `motivation` applies primarily to shared/multi-party chat rooms (`ARCHITECTURE.md` multi-party coordination); in single-user request-response or strict command-driven embodied loops where turns are allocated, `turn_allocated = true` keeps this field inert.

#### 10.3 `basis` (`observed` / `told` / `inferred` / `self`)
- **Empirical Evidence:** `Xu et al. (2026) [PP]` (`arXiv:2607.29167`) formalize **memory provenance laundering**: during LLM consolidation/reflection, untrusted external messages can be rewritten as apparent user history or verified rules, retaining action triggers while stripping low-trust origin (`ASR` up to **`1.000`** without immutable platform provenance; **`0`** unauthorized high-risk actions with a platform provenance firewall). `Turpin et al. (2023) [PR]` (`arXiv:2305.04388`) and `Lanham et al. (2023) [PP]` (`arXiv:2307.13702`) show CoT rationales routinely omit real causal drivers (`up to 36%` accuracy drop under unmentioned prompt bias). `Wang et al. (2026) [PP]` (`arXiv:2606.04990`) distinguish **system-observed execution provenance** from model-reported semantic justifications.
- **Deterministic Core Rule (Zero Self-Trust for `observed` & `self`):**
  Let each item in the log carry immutable platform-assigned metadata $(\text{origin\_kind}, \text{actor\_id}, \text{witnessed\_by\_soul})$. Decouple reasoning's `rests_on_ids[]` (supporting evidence IDs in `ContextPlan`) from `used_ids[]`:
  $$\text{basis}(b) = \begin{cases}
  \texttt{self} & \text{if generated by core } \texttt{R-09} \text{ agency matcher } (\text{never via } \texttt{think}),\\
  \texttt{told} & \text{if } b \text{ rests on } \ge 1 \text{ external speech/message percept } (\text{actor\_id} \neq \text{soul\_id}) \text{ and no ungrounded leap,}\\
  \texttt{observed} & \text{iff } \texttt{rests\_on\_ids} \neq \emptyset \;\land\; \forall id \in \texttt{rests\_on\_ids}:\; \text{reaches\_direct\_percept}(id) \;\land\; \neg\text{is\_message\_content}(id) \;\land\; \text{claimed}=\texttt{observed},\\
  \texttt{inferred} & \text{otherwise (including } \texttt{consolidate} \text{ gists/lessons, empty } \texttt{rests\_on\_ids}, \text{ or unverified } \texttt{observed} \text{ claims).}
  \end{cases}$$
  Crucially, per `beliefs/spec.md` L15, the event *"X said P"* carries `observed` (direct percept witnessed by the soul), whereas the extracted proposition *`P`* derived from X's utterance MUST carry **`told`** anchored to `told_by_actor_id = X` (preventing provenance laundering in `R-21` reliability updates!). Core **never** trusts a raw `basis = observed` or `basis = self` string from the LLM without verifying graph ancestry.
- **Fallback When Omitted:** `basis_fallback = inferred`. Note `design.md` L259 must add `rests_on_ids[]` (nullable array, or separate explicit `belief_writes[]` list with `{claim, rests_on_ids, claimed_basis, told_by_actor_id}`) so `think` internal monologue and grounded beliefs do not collide.

#### 10.4 `used_ids[]` (Self-Attribution / Context Citation)
- **Empirical Evidence:** Citation benchmarks show severe LLM unfaithfulness in attributing which context items produced a response: in `ALCE` (`Gao et al., 2023 [PR]` `arXiv:2305.14627`), `ChatGPT (5-psg Vanilla)` reaches only `73.6% / 72.5%` citation recall/precision on `ASQA`, **`51.1% / 50.0%`** on `ELI5`, and **`20.5% / 20.9%`** on `QAMPARI` (`GPT-4 20-psg`: `73.0 / 76.5`, `48.5 / 53.4`, `27.4 / 28.5`). Worse, `Wallat et al. (2025) [PR]` (`arXiv:2412.18004`, `doi:10.1145/3731120.3744592`) tested `Command-R++` on `NaturalQuestions` (`1,444` queries) and proved **`57%` (`273/476`)** post-rationalized citations when forging relevant-but-uncited passages and **`55%` (`290/525`)** on passages cited for unrelated reasons (`12%` / `116/936` even for random distractors). In `Agent Zero Memory` (`Wu & Zhu, 2026 [PP]` `arXiv:2608.29606`), structural **citation lock**—permitting citations *only* to evidence actually opened in the current retrieval window—eliminated citation fabrication and helped reach `95.60%` on `LongMemEval` and `93.60%` on `LoCoMo`.
- **Formula & Core Citation Lock:**
  Require `used_ids: string[]` (empty list `[]` allowed, never omitted as missing property). The perimeter assigns compact unambiguous cite tokens `[c1], [c2], ...` mapped 1-to-1 to the opaque IDs inside that wake's `ContextPlan`:
  $$\mathcal{U}_{\text{valid}} = \big(\text{map\_tokens}(\texttt{used\_ids})\big) \cap \mathcal{P}_{\text{ctx}}, \qquad r_{\text{phantom}} = \frac{|\texttt{used\_ids} \setminus \mathcal{P}_{\text{ctx}}|}{\max(1, |\texttt{used\_ids}|)}$$
  where $\mathcal{P}_{\text{ctx}}$ is the exact set of belief/memory/percept IDs rendered in `ContextPlan` for this wake.
  - Only items in $\mathcal{U}_{\text{valid}}$ receive Bayesian `(α, β)` credence increments upon outcome resolution (`R-20`); unrendered/fabricated IDs are stripped and logged via $r_{\text{phantom}}$.
  - **Omission / Empty `used_ids` Handling (`[]`):** Keep strict lock by default (updating nothing avoids unfairly penalizing/rewarding bystander memories that were retrieved into context but ignored), and run a Lab ablation comparing strict lock vs weighted retrieval fallback $w_i = 1 / |\mathcal{P}_{\text{retrieved}}|$ (`fallback_credit_mode ∈ {none, uniform_top_k}`) for wakes where the LLM omitted citations despite emitting a knowledge-grounded `act`.

#### 10.5 `value` & `cost` (for Report Control $P_{\text{rc}}$)
- **Empirical Evidence:** Classic strategic memory regulation (`Koriat & Goldsmith, 1996 [PR]` `doi:10.1037/0033-295x.103.3.490`) establishes that organisms monitor subjective confidence against a payoff-dependent criterion $P_{\text{rc}}$ determined by reward for correct report vs penalty for error. Modern cost-aware selective prediction (`Gupta, 2026 [PP]` `arXiv:2610.31657`, Eq. 2: $p c_{\text{ok}} + (1-p) c_{\text{wrong}} \le c_{\text{triage}} \implies p \ge 0.80$ under `(0, 1, 5)`; `Kotte, 2026 [PP]` `arXiv:2605.18796`; `Dong & Shinnou, 2026 [PP]` `arXiv:2607.04430`) confirms that `cost / (value + cost)` matches expected-utility break-even $\mathbb{E}[p V - (1-p) C] \ge 0 \iff p \ge \frac{C}{V+C}$. However, in all three modern systems, **the cost ratio is exogenous domain policy**, NOT self-reported as unconstrained floats by the same LLM producing the answer—for good reason: (a) if an uncalibrated LLM emits `value=100, cost=1`, it lowers $P_{\text{rc}} = 0.0099$ and bypasses report control (`Goodhart gaming`); (b) `value = cost = 0` produces `0 / 0 = NaN`; (c) if `P_rc` sits near `0.80` while raw/isotonic model confidence is lower, **the agent withholds almost everything** (`7/8` LLM settings in `Gupta, 2026`).
- **Formula & Elicitation:**
  Attach report control per **communicative `act`** (fixing the `think` vs `act` granularity conflict) using **core exogenous baseline stakes combined with a discrete bounded stakes tier** `stakes ∈ {low, medium, high, critical} | null` (`1..4` ordinal multiplier) or bounded `value, cost ∈ {1..10}` integers:
  $$V_{\text{eff}} = V_{\text{base}}(\text{place}, \text{cap}), \qquad C_{\text{eff}} = C_{\text{base}}(\text{place}, \text{cap}) \cdot m_{\text{stakes}}(\text{stakes}) \cdot (1 + \eta_{\text{promise}}\mathbb{I}[\text{linked\_promise}])$$
  $$P_{\text{rc}} = \max\!\left(\text{certainty\_bar},\; \frac{\max(C_{\text{eff}}, 0)}{\max(V_{\text{eff}} + C_{\text{eff}},\, \epsilon_{\text{rc}})}\right)$$
  with default mapping $m_{\text{stakes}} \in \{0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 4.0\}$ (`medium = 1.0`) and neutral domain defaults $V_{\text{base}} = 1.0, C_{\text{base}} = 1.0 \implies \frac{C}{V+C} = 0.50$. If `design.md` retains numeric `(value, cost)`, constrain schema to integers `[1, 10]` (nullable -> default `value = 1, cost = 1`) and guard division with $\epsilon_{\text{rc}} = 10^{-6}$.
- **Deterministic Core Computation:** Whether an answer fulfills an active `promise` (`linked_promise`) and the place/capability's baseline criticality `(V_base, C_base)` are 100% deterministic in core without relying on self-report. *Body-specificity note:* Coarsening ("exact time -> day -> period; name -> someone in group") in `R-15` is **chat/verbal-report specific** (`reasoning/spec.md` L142); for embodied/actuator capabilities, failing $c < P_{\text{rc}}$ should recall or withhold/defer (`act` withheld), NOT emit a "coarser" motor action unless the capability explicitly declares safe degraded parameters.

#### 10.6 `expected` (Expected Reply Text on `act`)
- **Empirical Evidence:** No verified 2023–2026 study validates `miss = 1 - max(cos(reply, act), cos(reply, expected))` as a calibrated detector of conversational repair (`Ngo et al., 2026 [PR]` `2026.lrec-1.547` studies human-vs-LLM conversational repair annotation, highlighting the subtlety of conversational repair acts). Dense embedding cosine suffers from known failure modes on short conversational turns and negation/clarification requests (`"Sure, works for me"` vs `"Wait, what do you mean?"` can both show low lexical/embedding similarity to a long multi-clause `act`).
- **Elicitation, Privacy Boundary, & Fallback:** Elicit `expected: string | null` **only on response-expecting communicative `act`s** (`expect_response = true`), asking for a concise (`<= 160` char) canonical summary of an ordinary positive/on-topic reply (NEVER required on internal/physical non-conversational `act`s). The perimeter embeds `expected` via the configured embedder (`vec(expected)`) and **passes only the vector into core** (`ARCHITECTURE.md`: core never reads text!). When `expected` is omitted (`null`), core drops `cos(reply, expected)` cleanly so `miss = 1 - cos(reply, act)` (`design.md` L246). Core **cannot** synthesize `expected` without an LLM, so empirical Lab validation of `cos(reply, expected)` vs `cos(reply, act)` + structural features (`reply_length`, `latency_share`) is mandatory.

#### 10.7 `importance` ($i_{\text{reason}} \in [0, 1]$)
- **Empirical Evidence:** `Park et al. (2023) [PR]` (`arXiv:2304.03442`, `doi:10.1145/3586183.3606763`) introduced a 1–10 poignancy rating prompt; full architecture achieved TrueSkill $\mu = 29.89\ (\sigma = 0.72)$ vs no-reflection ($\mu = 26.88$), no-reflection/planning ($\mu = 25.64$), crowdworker ($\mu = 22.95$), and no-memory/plan/reflect ($\mu = 21.21$, Kruskal-Wallis $H(4) = 150.29, p < 0.001$). **Crucial limitation:** Park et al. **never ablated `importance` independently**, nor reported calibration between rated poignancy and downstream retrieval utility. Later evidence (`Annapureddy & Thamatani, 2026 [PP]` `arXiv:2609.33013`, Phase 2) shows arbitrary retention heuristics under finite memory budgets lead to budget bloat and transfer failures, whereas structural surprise and downstream utility signals drive `+22.7%` task-success gains at `7.17x` compression.
- **Formula & Fallback:** Core already computes deterministic `i_commit = 1.0` (commitment-linked) and `i_surprise = clamp(z / z_max, 0, 1)`. Elicit `importance` as a nullable **integer `1..10 | null`** (`"1 = routine chatter, 10 = life/goal-defining event"`, anchored to `Park et al., 2023`) normalized via:
  $$i_{\text{reason}} = \begin{cases}
  w_{\text{reason}} \cdot \frac{\text{importance} - 1}{S_{\text{imp}} - 1} & \text{if } \text{importance} \neq \texttt{null},\\
  0.0 & \text{if omitted / } \texttt{null},
  \end{cases}
  \qquad I = \max\!\big(i_{\text{reason}},\, i_{\text{commit}},\, i_{\text{surprise}}\big)$$
  with $S_{\text{imp}} = 10$ and gate weight $w_{\text{reason}} = 1.0$ (set $w_{\text{reason}} = 0.0$ if Lab ablation shows LLM `importance` adds noise or overrides `i_surprise`). Notice omission cleanly evaluates `max(0, i_commit, i_surprise)` with zero failure.

#### 10.8 `domain` Label (on `think` / `belief`)
- **Empirical Evidence & Invariant Violation:** Allowing LLMs to invent free-text domain labels (`"scheduling"`, `"calendar"`, `"appointments"`) breaks `ARCHITECTURE.md` ("core sees only opaque ids") and fractures `(actor, domain)` Beta counts `(α, β)` across sparse synonymous keys (`3%–21%` out-of-range/schema drift on non-standard domains, `Dai & Wang, 2026`). Core already computes $K$ vector cluster centroids deterministically during sleep (`design.md` L304, A-12) or accepts an external gateway channel/capability `domain_id`.
- **Rule & Fallback:** Default rule: **reasoning supplies NO domain field**; core deterministically sets `domain_id = domain_from_gateway ?? argmin_k (1 - cos(v_item, centroid_k))`. If an optional override flag `allow_llm_domain_override = true` is tested in Lab, `ContextPlan` injects only the candidate opaque `domain_id`s with centroid exemplar summaries, and `think` emits `domain_id ∈ ContextPlan.active_domain_ids | null` (any unknown string rejected and replaced by nearest centroid).

### Discrepancies & Gaps Found in `core-and-lab` (For Follow-up OpenSpec Change)

1. **`domain` missing from Decision 9 while required by Decision 10 & `beliefs/spec.md`:** `design.md` L305 and `beliefs/spec.md` L80 state a domain may be *"an id supplied from outside or by reasoning"*, yet `think(...)`, `revise(...)`, and the other 3 thought acts in Decision 9 (`design.md` L259–266) have **no `domain` parameter**. Fix: either drop "or by reasoning" (recommended, core centroid + external capability domain only) or add `domain_id: DomainId | null` restricted to active centroid IDs.
2. **`think` vs `act` granularity conflict on `confidence`, `value`, `cost`:** `reasoning/spec.md` L139 attaches `value` and `cost` to **"an answer"** (a communicative `act`), yet `design.md` L259 puts `confidence, value?, cost?` on `think(...)`. If one wake issues multiple `act`s or emits a belief `think` alongside an `act` (`model-providers/spec.md` L30 allows any combination of acts, including `act` without `think`!), per-act report control `P_rc` is ambiguous or missing `confidence/value/cost`, and `value = cost = 0` produces `0/0` `NaN`.
3. **Contradiction between `design.md` Decision 9 and `reasoning/spec.md` on required vs conditional fields:** `design.md` L259 makes `motivation 1–5` and `confidence` unconditional on `think(...)`, whereas `reasoning/spec.md` L125 requires them specifically *"when the soul takes a turn that was not allocated to it"* (while `P_rc` also needs `confidence` on answers).
4. **Single `used_ids[]` conflates belief provenance (`basis`) with decision attribution (`R-20`):** `beliefs/spec.md` L14 requires *"Reasoning SHALL state the basis with the events it rests on"*, while `design.md` L298 uses `used_ids` to update credence of beliefs relied on in `think`. A communicative decision relies on existing beliefs (`used_ids[]`), whereas forming a new belief relies on source percepts/messages (`rests_on_ids[]`, `told_by_actor_id`). Splitting them fixes provenance verification cleanly.
5. **Non-strict optional parameter syntax breaks OpenAI Structured Outputs (`strict: true`):** Marking `importance?, basis?, value?, cost?, expected?` as optional JSON properties fails `strict: true` providers (`Geng et al., 2025`); schemas must require all keys with explicit `| null` unions (`"anyOf": [..., {"type": "null"}]`).
6. **Scale mismatch on `importance` (`R-05`):** `design.md` L202 treats `i_reason ∈ [0, 1]`, while `Park et al. (2023)` prompts `1..10` integers without specifying the prompt scale or rescaling rule in `design.md`.

## Neutral value

Two levels of neutrality apply cleanly so the loop always remains alive and deterministic:
1. **Mechanism neutral values (disables gating / self-report reliance while loop runs):**
   - Turn bars off: `speaking_bar = 1`, `certainty_bar = 0.0` (every valid act executes immediately).
   - Report control off: `P_rc_enabled = false` (`V_base = 1.0, C_base = 0.0 \implies P_rc = 0.0`, no recall/coarsen/withhold loop).
   - Calibration map identity: $f_{\text{cal}, m}(c) = c$.
   - Credence attribution neutral: `credence_update = off` (no $(\alpha, \beta)$ shifts from `used_ids`).
   - Reasoned importance neutral: `w_reason_imp = 0.0` (`importance = max(0, i_commit, i_surprise)`).
   - Expected reply neutral: `expected = null` (`miss = 1 - cos(reply, act)`).
   - Domain neutral: `K = 1` single global domain (`domain_id = domain_global`).
2. **Per-field omission fallbacks when a model outputs `null`:** `confidence -> 0.0` on unallocated/P_rc (fail-closed) or `0.5` non-communicative; `motivation -> 1` on unallocated (fail-closed) / ignored on allocated; `basis -> inferred`; `used_ids -> []`; `(value, cost) -> (1.0, 1.0)`; `expected -> null` (uses `vec(act)` only); `importance -> 0.0`; `domain_id -> nearest centroid`.

## Claim

Replacing unconstrained LLM self-report with strict nullable schemas, `[0, 20]` coupled confidence (+ rationale), deterministic graph-verified `basis`, `ContextPlan` citation-locked `used_ids[]`, bounded `P_rc` stakes, and centroid-assigned `domain` reduces provenance-laundering & phantom-ID corruption to zero (`phantom_id_rate = 0`, unauthorized high-trust `observed` promotion = `0`), improves risk-coverage calibration (`ECE` and `meta-d'` over `[0, 100]` or `[0, 1]` floats), eliminates strict-mode schema drops, and avoids `100%` abstention collapse on `P_rc` and `importance` budget bloat.

## How the Lab would test it

Using the discrete-event virtual-time Lab (`specs/lab/spec.md`) with seeded determinism and scripted/live calibration benchmarks:
1. **Confidence scale & anti-collapse ablation:** Compare `0–20 + rationale` vs `0–100` vs `0–1` float vs constant-collapsed scripted provider (`std = 0`) across calibration scenarios (`Brier`, `ECE`, `AUROC`, `meta-d'`, unallocated over-speaking rate, and `P_rc` false-withholding rate). Test whether post-hoc isotonic/Platt recalibration resolves `GPT-OSS`-style raw-miscalibrated high-AUROC behavior without collapsing coverage (`Gupta, 2026`).
2. **Provenance attack & citation-lock test:** Inject hostile external actor messages claiming direct observations and instruct/perturb model `think` calls to emit `basis = observed` and cite out-of-context IDs. Measure: (a) fraction of ungrounded `observed` claims caught and downgraded to `inferred` (`target = 100%`), (b) `phantom_id_rate` rejected by `ContextPlan` intersection (`target = 0%` entering credence updates).
3. **Credence attribution comparison (`used_ids`):** Run multi-turn source-reliability scenarios comparing `used_ids ∩ ContextPlan` vs `uniform_top_k` fallback when `used_ids = []` vs unverified self-report on `(actor, domain)` reliability convergence speed and bystander blame rate.
4. **Report control `P_rc` stakes sweep:** Compare ordinal `stakes_tier` + core exogenous `(V_base, C_base)` vs unconstrained numeric `(value, cost)` floats across low- and high-risk factual tasks (verifying zero `NaN`/Goodhart gaming and checking selective risk-coverage AURC rather than total abstention).
5. **`i_reason` & `expected` ablations (`R-05` & `R-18`):** Run memory retrieval/forgetting scenarios with `w_reason_imp = 1.0` vs `0.0` (measuring `recall_hit@k` and token/item budget overhead), and run chat repair scenarios comparing `miss = 1 - max(cos(reply, act), cos(reply, expected))` vs `1 - cos(reply, act)` vs adding reply-length/latency features on false-positive and false-negative repair F1.
6. **Structured output strictness test:** Replay all 5 thought-act schemas through OpenAI `strict: true` and small open-weight models measuring first-pass valid parse rate, retry count (`N_reask`), and null-fallback invocation rate.

## Established / inferred / open

- **Established:**
  - `0–20` integer scale maximizes `meta-d'` and `M_ratio` across models while `0–100` heavily discretizes (`78.2%–92.1%` Top-3) (`Dai & Wang, 2026 [PP]`); coupled 1-step confidence beats 2-step CoT confidence (`Tian et al., 2023 [PR]`); brief uncertainty rationales prevent constant-value confidence collapse and raw signals frequently need per-model post-hoc calibration (`Cao et al., 2026 [PP]`; `Gupta, 2026 [PP]`).
  - LLM citations and CoT explanations are unfaithful: `55%–57%` post-rationalized citations (`Wallat et al., 2025 [PR]`), `20.5%–73.6%` ALCE recall (`Gao et al., 2023 [PR]`), up to `36%` accuracy distortion via unmentioned CoT bias (`Turpin et al., 2023 [PR]`), and up to `1.000` attack success rate from memory provenance laundering without platform-maintained origin tracking (`Xu et al., 2026 [PP]`). Structural citation locking (`Wu & Zhu, 2026 [PP]`) eliminates fabricated citations.
  - Positive-only motivation prompts systematically overrate; eliciting positive + negative factors curbs over-speaking (`Liu et al., 2025 [PR]`).
  - Non-required schema keys fail OpenAI `strict: true` decoding (`Geng et al., 2025 [PP]`; `OpenAI Docs [IND]`), and valid JSON syntax still suffers `>=17%` leaf-value errors on text (`Singh et al., 2026 [PP]`).
- **Inferred:**
  - Splitting `rests_on_ids[]` (belief provenance) from `used_ids[]` (decision credence), relocating answer `(confidence, value, cost)` onto communicative `act`s, and mapping discrete `stakes_tier` onto core exogenous `(V_base, C_base)` preserves all three mechanisms (`R-15`, `R-17`, `R-20`) while obeying every `ARCHITECTURE.md` invariant.
  - Using deterministic core `max(i_commit, i_surprise)` and nearest-centroid `domain_id` by default prevents subjective numeric distortion without adding LLM cost.
- **Open:**
  - **Not settled by the literature:** Whether LLM-supplied `i_reason` (`R-05`) improves downstream recall/success over deterministic `max(i_commit, i_surprise)` alone (`Park et al., 2023` never ablated `importance` independently); settle via Lab `i_reason` ablation.
  - **Not settled by the literature:** Whether `1 - max(cos(reply, act), cos(reply, expected))` (`R-18`) reliably separates conversational repair from short affirmative acknowledgments, and which `(V_base, C_base)` tiers optimize risk-coverage under `P_rc` across non-expert models; settle via Lab chat-repair and stakes-sweep experiments.
  - **Not settled by the literature:** Whether standalone 1–5 integer `motivation` without token logprobs preserves `Liu et al. (2025)` turn appropriateness across diverse backbones.

# A-11 Lessons and procedures

**Status:** partly answered

## Answer

During sleep consolidation (`consolidate`), **detection of shared prediction errors and extraction of candidate action chains are 100% deterministic and content-free inside the core**, while **`consolidate` writes the natural-language lesson and procedure schema only from the bundled IDs**. Specifically: (1) **shared prediction error** across $\ge 2$ distinct episodes is detected in core by exact structural matching on immutable opaque IDs `(error_kind, expect_kind, cap_id)` combined with composite vector cosine + optional normalized error-magnitude proximity + soft `place_id`/`actor_id` affinity ($S_{\text{err}} \ge \tau_{\text{err}} = 0.72$, cosine component $\ge 0.68$, or exact `(place_id/actor_id, cap_id, error_kind, magnitude_bin)` match without vectors), plus pairing up to $M_{\text{contrast}} = 2$ nearby successful episodes sharing the same capability for contrastive `lesson` synthesis; (2) **procedure segmentation** anchors on a terminal resolved `goal` event (`outcome ∈ {reached, kept}`) and deterministically walks `caused_by` / `goal_id` links across wakes and places (NOT trapped inside single per-place episode slices), pruning dead-end failed retries while preserving distinct recovery capabilities as optional `on_fail` fallback branches; (3) procedures are abstracted into **typed slot templates + ordered `cap_id` sequences**, indexed via multi-key goal/precondition embeddings and required capability IDs, scored and retrieved using Petrov base-level activation coupled with **Bayesian Beta credence** `(α, β)` + expected utility, and **revised or soft-retired (`superseded`/`retired`)** rather than budget-evicted when post-promotion failures or poison decoys occur.

### Part 1: Detecting "Shared Prediction Error" Without Reading Content & Writing Lesson Text

#### 11.1 Opaque Core Error Signature (Zero Content Reading)
Every failed prediction or unmet expectation in the MonoAgens event log (`expectations/spec.md`, `protocol/spec.md`, `design.md` Decisions 8–9) already provides structured non-linguistic fields and perimeter-computed vectors. Define the opaque signature of error event $e_i$ as:
$$\kappa(e_i) = \big(\text{error\_kind}_i,\; \text{expect\_kind}_i,\; \text{cap\_id}_i,\; \text{place\_id}_i,\; \text{actor\_id}_i,\; z_{\text{err}, i},\; \mathbf{v}_{\text{trig}, i},\; \mathbf{v}_{\text{err}, i}\big)$$
where:
- `error_kind ∈ {effect_unmet, effect_mismatch, response_timeout, response_content_miss, response_silence, promise_failed, goal_failed, goal_expired}` (deterministic enum from core comparators `R-08`, `R-09`, `R-18`).
- `expect_kind ∈ {effect, response, wait, promise, goal}`.
- `cap_id`: opaque capability ID of the causative `act` (or `null` for passive `wait`/`goal`).
- `place_id`, `actor_id`: opaque place and counterparty actor IDs (optional affinity, NOT hard equality locks, so cross-place lessons still form).
- $z_{\text{err}, i} \ge 0$: standardized surprise / timing / content-miss $z$-score at failure time (`0` if binary receipt failure).
- $\mathbf{v}_{\text{trig}, i} \in \mathbb{R}^d$: embedding vector of the triggering context + `act` (`null` in non-text sensor/robot bodies).
- $\mathbf{v}_{\text{err}, i} \in \mathbb{R}^d$: embedding vector of the unexpected reply or failed state (`null` on timeouts/silence or vectorless bodies).

#### 11.2 Deterministic Cross-Episode Error Equivalence & Clustering Formula
For two distinct episodes $E_a \neq E_b$ containing error events $e_i \in E_a$ and $e_j \in E_b$ (drawn from unconsolidated errors + archived unresolved cluster exemplars):

1. **Hard Structural Equivalence Gate (mandatory):**
   $$\mathbb{I}_{\text{struct}}(e_i, e_j) = \mathbb{I}\!\big[\text{error\_kind}_i = \text{error\_kind}_j \;\land\; \text{expect\_kind}_i = \text{expect\_kind}_j \;\land\; \text{cap\_id}_i = \text{cap\_id}_j\big]$$
   (Two errors with *different* error kinds, expectation kinds, or capabilities—e.g. an actuator `effect_unmet` vs a chat `response_timeout`—are **never** merged as the same prediction error.)

2. **Similarity Score (Vector Bodies vs Vectorless Embodied/Sensor Bodies):**
   - When embeddings exist for both items (at least $\mathbf{v}_{\text{trig}}$), compute:
     $$S_{\text{vec}}(e_i, e_j) = w_{\text{trig}}\,\cos(\mathbf{v}_{\text{trig}, i}, \mathbf{v}_{\text{trig}, j}) + (1 - w_{\text{trig}})\,\cos_{\text{avail}}(\mathbf{v}_{\text{err}, i}, \mathbf{v}_{\text{err}, j})$$
     where $\cos_{\text{avail}} = \cos(\mathbf{v}_{\text{err}, i}, \mathbf{v}_{\text{err}, j})$ if both error vectors exist, otherwise falls back cleanly to $\cos(\mathbf{v}_{\text{trig}, i}, \mathbf{v}_{\text{trig}, j})$, and $w_{\text{trig}} = 0.50$. Require component cosine $S_{\text{vec}} \ge \theta_{\text{err\_cos}} = 0.68$ (slightly below MACLA's $\theta_{\text{dup}} = 0.85$ duplicate-procedure threshold because distinct failure episodes differ in surface context while sharing the underlying trap).
   - Compute total composite affinity (including place/actor/magnitude signals):
     $$S_{\text{err}}(e_i, e_j) = S_{\text{vec}}(e_i, e_j) + \eta_{\text{place}}\mathbb{I}[\text{place}_i=\text{place}_j] + \eta_{\text{actor}}\mathbb{I}[\text{actor}_i=\text{actor}_j] - \eta_z \frac{|z_{\text{err}, i} - z_{\text{err}, j}|}{\sigma_z}$$
     Match holds (vector mode) iff $\mathbb{I}_{\text{struct}} = 1 \;\land\; S_{\text{vec}} \ge \theta_{\text{err\_cos}} \;\land\; S_{\text{err}} \ge \tau_{\text{err}}$ (start $\tau_{\text{err}} = 0.72$, search `[0.55, 0.88]`).
   - **Vectorless / Embodied Sensor Fallback (`model-providers/spec.md` allows embeddings optional; `ARCHITECTURE.md` generic across bodies):** When $\mathbf{v} = \texttt{null}$, match holds deterministically iff:
     $$\mathbb{I}_{\text{struct}}(e_i, e_j) = 1 \;\land\; \big(\text{place}_i = \text{place}_j \;\lor\; \text{actor}_i = \text{actor}_j\big) \;\land\; \exp\!\left(-\frac{|z_{\text{err}, i} - z_{\text{err}, j}|}{\sigma_z}\right) \ge \theta_{\text{mag}}$$
     (start $\theta_{\text{mag}} = 0.50$, $\sigma_z = 1.0$).

3. **Deterministic Replayable Clustering & Contrast Selection:**
   Sort all eligible errors in canonical order `(timestamp, log_seq, event_id)` and run single-pass deterministic **leader clustering** against cluster centroids `(mean_vec_trig, mean_vec_err, key)` (no randomized initialization, zero hash-map order dependence). When a cluster reaches $|C_{\text{distinct\_episodes}}| \ge N_{\text{err\_min}} = 2$:
   - Core checks recent volatility/instability (`2609.33013` G1): retain episode IDs cleanly and fetch up to $M_{\text{contrast}} = 2$ historical **successful** episodes sharing `(cap_id)` (and $\cos(\mathbf{v}_{\text{trig}}, \mathbf{c}_{\text{cluster}}) \ge 0.65$, or same `place_id` without vectors) so reasoning sees **both why it failed $\ge 2$ times and what succeeded in a similar context** (`MACLA` Sec. 4.3; `ExpeL`).

#### 11.3 How `consolidate` Writes the Lesson Text
Core dispatches one `consolidate` job per triggered cluster (within sleep budget, `sleep-consolidation/spec.md` L65), passing the perimeter-rendered texts of the $\ge 2$ matching failure episodes + error receipts + up to $M_{\text{contrast}} = 2$ contrast successes, alongside any existing active lesson on the same `(cap_id, error_kind)` whose centroid similarity $\ge 0.75$. Reasoning returns a structured `record_lesson` consolidation act (fixing `design.md` L288):
- **`situation`:** concise abstracted precondition / warning pattern (abstracted slots, no over-fitted literal names unless actor/place-specific).
- **`action`:** the problematic action choice (plus what the contrast episode did differently when available).
- **`outcome`:** observed failure mode + recommended preventive guard.
- **Core-enforced invariants on storage:** Core rejects or strips any `source_episode_ids` / `source_error_ids` not actually in the cluster bundle passed to `consolidate` (zero provenance laundering, `Xu et al., 2026`), forces `basis = inferred`, assigns nearest `domain_id` centroid, sets initial Beta credence $(\alpha_L, \beta_L) = (2, 1)$ (weighted prior reflecting $\ge 2$ verified failure occurrences confirming the hazard, `ExpeL` starts importance at `2`) or `(1, 1)` neutral prior, and records `supersedes_lesson_id` if refining an earlier lesson (`Mem^p` Adjustment).
- **How Lesson Credence Updates:** When a lesson $L$ is retrieved (`L.id ∈ used_ids_valid`) on a subsequent wake involving `(cap_id, expect_kind)`:
  - If the wake resolves **without** repeating `L.error_kind` (success / avoided failure): $\alpha_L \leftarrow \alpha_L + 1$.
  - If the same `L.error_kind` recurs *despite* $L$ being cited in `used_ids_valid`, OR if a subsequent wake contradicts $L$ cleanly (`revise`): $\beta_L \leftarrow \beta_L + 1$. (Relaxed per period toward $(\alpha_0, \beta_0)$ with decay $\lambda_{\text{proc}}$.)

### Part 2: Procedure Extraction, Segmentation, Parameterization, Credence & Retirement

#### 11.4 `core-and-lab` Contradictions Blocking Procedure Extraction Today
Before specifying the rule, three concrete conflicts in `core-and-lab` MUST be noted:
1. **`consolidate` lacks structured product acts (`design.md` L288 vs Decision 11 & `sleep-consolidation/spec.md` L95–98):** `design.md` restricts `consolidate` to `think` and `revise` only, neither of which carries `record_lesson(situation, action, outcome, source_ids)` or `record_procedure(skill_name, preconditions, steps[], param_slots[], goal_id, source_ids)`.
2. **Outcome enum mismatch (`sleep-consolidation/spec.md` L105 vs `design.md` L263):** `sleep-consolidation/spec.md` triggers procedure extraction when a goal is resolved as **`"reached"`**, whereas `design.md` defines `resolve(id, outcome ∈ {kept, failed, cancelled, answered})` (omitting `"reached"`) and `expectations/spec.md` L187 lists *"resolved, answered, expired or revised away"*. Canonical fix: unify `goal` resolution outcomes as `{reached, failed, cancelled}` (mapping legacy `kept -> reached`).
3. **Single per-place episode premise (`sleep-consolidation/spec.md` L25 vs `lab/spec.md` L154 & `design.md` L234–240):** `sleep-consolidation/spec.md` L25 splits events strictly **per place**, whereas `lab/spec.md` L154 explicitly evaluates *"a multi-step task across two places"* and `design.md` tracks cross-place goals via `caused_by` edges. Slicing procedures from a single per-place episode severs cross-place workflows!

#### 11.5 Procedure Segmentation & Filtering (Cross-Episode Goal-Chain Tracing)
Rather than relying purely on unanchored text segmentation (in `Annapureddy & Thamatani, 2026`, unsupervised `BoundaryLearner` reached `0.691` oracle correlation, below the `0.70` target and slightly behind Bayesian surprise on task success `0.679 vs 0.705`), MonoAgens already logs explicit **goal opening and resolution events**:
1. **Deterministic Causal Span Extraction:** Whenever consolidation processes a `GoalResolved(outcome = reached)` event $e_{\text{end}}$, the core traces backward deterministically through explicit `(goal_id, expect_id, caused_by)` edges (cycle-guarded BFS/DFS bounded by horizon $H_{\text{proc}} = 50$ events, sorted canonically by `(timestamp, log_seq)`) to the root `GoalOpened` / `expect(goal)` event $e_{\text{start}}$, spanning multiple wakes and places cleanly. (If no explicit `goal` event links to any `act`, core does not emit speculative procedures.)
2. **Handling Failed / Retried Steps inside the Trace:**
   Every `act` node $a_t$ in the traced chain carries its deterministic `receipt` outcome (`applied` vs `rejected`/`failed`/`timed_out`) and linked `effect`/`response` resolution (`kept` vs `failed`/`miss`):
   - **Rule A — Strip dead-end & redundant retries (`keep successful subsequence`):** If $a_t$ had receipt `rejected`/`failed` or unmet effect and was immediately followed (same `(cap_id, args_shape)` with no intervening external state advance) by a succeeding call to the same `cap_id`, **discard** the failed attempt cleanly (matching `AWM` App. B step pruning & `Mem^p` trajectory distillation).
   - **Rule B — Retain state-changing recovery branches as annotated `on_fail` edges (`annotated failure branch`):** If $a_t$ failed (`effect_unmet` / `response_miss`) and the trace pivoted to a **different capability or repaired argument pattern** $a_{\text{recover}}$ (`cap_recover != cap_failed` or distinct parameter class) before advancing to reach the goal, **do NOT flatten the failure as a mandatory step**; instead emit primary step `s` with an optional conditional metadata branch `s.on_fail = {error_kind, recovery_step_ref}` (or extract the recovery path as a reusable branch only if `recover` succeeded). Keep final extracted trace bounded to $L_{\text{proc\_max}} = 12$ abstract steps.

#### 11.6 Slot Parameterization, Cross-Body Handling, & Multi-Key Indexing
1. **Structural Skeleton & `consolidate` Abstraction:** Core first generates the deterministic structural trace `[(step_idx, cap_id, place_role, expect_kind, outcome_class, on_fail_tag)]` and computes a canonical `cap_seq_hash`. Only if `(cap_seq_hash)` is non-trivial ($\ge 2$ steps or single high-latency/cross-place capability) and not already consolidated, core dispatches the traced segment (plus up to $M_{\text{contrast}} = 2$ failed attempts on the same `goal` pattern if present) to `consolidate` via `record_procedure`:
   - Replaces concrete literals (`actor_id`, `place_id`, message strings, IDs, dates) with typed slot placeholders (`{target_actor}`, `{target_place}`, `{query}`, `{resource_id}`, matching `AWM`).
   - Emits concise `skill_name`, `precondition` summary, `goal` summary, and per-step `intent` + `arg_slots` description (`MACLA` $\langle \mathcal{G}_k, \Psi_k, \pi_k, \Phi_k \rangle$).
   - **Generic across bodies note:** On embodied/code/tool-API bodies (`Voyager`, `SkillWeaver`), capabilities carry strict JSON argument schemas and deterministic `effect` receipts, enabling mechanical slot validation; in pure chat bodies (`AWM` dialogue tasks), steps represent communicative intentions + expected response kinds with higher parameter variability (`SkillWeaver` found identifying the right skill vs binding wrong parameters were the two top failure modes).
2. **Multi-Key Skill Indexing (`Mem^p` Evidence):** `Mem^p` showed `Key=AveFact` (averaged key-element embeddings) outperformed raw `Key=Query` on TravelPlanner (`76.02 vs 73.38` CS, GPT-4o). Store procedures with three complementary deterministic indices:
   - `v_goal`: embedding of abstracted goal description (computed at perimeter).
   - `v_pre`: embedding of precondition + initial trigger context (`centroid_init`).
   - `required_cap_ids[]`: opaque set of capability IDs (lets core pre-filter any procedure requiring a capability currently absent/ungranted on the current body!).

#### 11.7 Credence Scoring, Retrieval Ranking, Revision & Retirement
1. **Initial Credence & Online Bayesian Updates (Grounded in `MACLA` + `R-20` + Citation Lock):**
   - At extraction from a verified `reached` goal trace, initialize $(\alpha_p, \beta_p) = (\alpha_{\text{seed}}, \beta_{\text{seed}}) = (2, 1)$ (or neutral $(1, 1)$ before deployment).
   - Maintain **Bayesian reliability mean and uncertainty**:
     $$\hat{\rho}_p = \frac{\alpha_p}{\alpha_p + \beta_p}, \qquad \sigma_p = \sqrt{\frac{\alpha_p \beta_p}{(\alpha_p + \beta_p)^2 (\alpha_p + \beta_p + 1)}}$$
   - During explicit/automatic recall (`memory-recall/spec.md`), rank candidate procedures `p` that pass the body capability filter (`required_cap_ids ⊆ active_caps`) by combining Petrov base-level activation $B_p$ and semantic similarity with `MACLA`-style expected-utility score (`Eq. 4`):
     $$U_{\text{proc}}(p \mid \text{cue}) = S_{\text{cue}}(p) \cdot \Big(\hat{\rho}_p \cdot R_{\text{proc}} - (1 - \hat{\rho}_p) \cdot C_{\text{proc}}\Big) + \lambda_{\text{info}} \cdot \sigma_p + w_{\text{act}} B_p$$
     where $S_{\text{cue}}(p) = \frac{1}{2}\cos(\mathbf{v}_{\text{cue}}, \mathbf{v}_{\text{goal}, p}) + \frac{1}{2}\cos(\mathbf{v}_{\text{cue}}, \mathbf{v}_{\text{pre}, p})$ and $\lambda_{\text{info}} \cdot \sigma_p$ provides bounded exploration for newly learned procedures (start $R_{\text{proc}} = 1.0, C_{\text{proc}} = 0.5, \lambda_{\text{info}} = 0.10$; cf. `MACLA` App. D.1.2).
   - **Strict Attribution Rule:** Update $\alpha_p \leftarrow \alpha_p + 1$ on subsequent goal `reached` and $\beta_p \leftarrow \beta_p + 1$ on subsequent `failed`/`expired` **only when `p.id ∈ used_ids ∩ ContextPlan.rendered_ids`** (`citation lock`), preventing passive bystander bloat. Periodically relax counts toward prior $(\alpha_0, \beta_0)$ with decay $\lambda_{\text{proc}}$ (`0.95` per consolidation epoch, `R-20`) so outdated workflows decay toward `0.50` uncertainty rather than holding infinite stale weight.
2. **Duplicate Merging, Contrastive Revision, & Safe Retirement (Anti-Poisoning):**
   - **Merge duplicates:** On new extraction, if an active procedure $p'$ shares the same canonical `cap_seq_hash` AND $\cos(\mathbf{v}_{\text{goal}, p_{\text{new}}}, \mathbf{v}_{\text{goal}, p'}) \ge \theta_{\text{proc\_dup}} = 0.85$ (`MACLA` Sec. 4.6), merge source episode provenance links into $p'$ and increment $\alpha_{p'} \leftarrow \alpha_{p'} + 1$ (`MACLA` compressed `2,851` traces to `187` procedures via `θ_dup = 0.85`).
   - **Contrastive Adjustment vs Supersession:** When a recalled procedure $p$ contributes to failed episodes ($n_{\text{used}} = (\alpha_p - \alpha_0) + (\beta_p - \beta_0) \ge n_{\text{proc\_min\_trials}} = 3$) and either its posterior mean falls below $\theta_{\text{proc\_revise}} = 0.50$ or an updated success trace succeeds with a modified sequence, queue `(p, S_p, F_p)` to `consolidate` for **contrastive adjustment** (`Mem^p` Adjustment; `MACLA` Sec. 4.3), producing `revise(target_id = p.id, new_version)` (`supersedes_id = p.id`, tightening preconditions $\Psi$ or updating a step).
   - **Retirement / Rollback (`2609.33013` G1–G2):** If $\hat{\rho}_p$ (or lower 95% Beta quantile) drops below $\theta_{\text{proc\_retire}} = 0.30$ after $\ge n_{\text{proc\_min\_trials}}$ failures (e.g., an API/environment rule changed or a poison decoy broke), core marks `status = retired` (which suppresses default recall alongside `superseded` per `memory-recall/spec.md` L103 while keeping full provenance and source logs intact for G2 rollback & audit). **NEVER hard-delete procedures purely from token/item budget pressure** (`2609.33013` Phase 2 proved chance eviction destroys `74%` of load-bearing clusters and causes `-15.0 pp` transfer regression).

## Neutral value

- `N_err_min = ∞` (`lesson_extraction = off`): sleep produces episodic gists only, matching no-lesson consolidation.
- `procedure_extraction = off` (`H_proc = 0`): no procedures are segmented, indexed, or recalled (equivalent to `Voyager w/o Skill Library` / `Mem^p No Memory`).
- `theta_proc_retire = 0.0`, `theta_proc_revise = 0.0`, `lambda_proc = 1.0`, `lambda_info = 0.0`: stored procedures remain static without adaptive revision, decay, or uncertainty bonus, while the cognitive loop continues normally on raw episodes and semantic gists.

## Claim

1. **Lessons:** Deterministic opaque-ID + vector clustering triggers contrastive lesson synthesis whenever an avoidable error repeats ($\ge 2$ episodes), reducing `repeat_error_rate` on recurring traps across places/actors without inspecting text content or admitting fabricated provenance.
2. **Procedures:** Goal-chain tracing across places with retry-stripping, slot parameterization, multi-key skill indexing, and Beta-credence ranking increases `goal_success_rate` and lowers `steps_to_goal` (`Mem^p`, `AWM`, `MACLA`, `Voyager`, `SkillWeaver`), while contrastive revision and thresholded soft retirement prevent stale-skill lock-in and poison-decoy transfer collapse (`0` false rules, full rollback recovery, `Annapureddy & Thamatani, 2026`).

## How the Lab would test it

1. **Cross-place recurring-trap test (`lesson` ablation):** Run scenarios where a non-obvious capability precondition or partner communication norm fails across $\ge 2$ distinct episodes (and optionally different places) while succeeding under an alternate precondition. Compare full lesson clustering + `M_contrast=2` vs failure-only lessons vs no-lesson baseline (`N_err_min = ∞`) on `repeat_error_rate` after sleep consolidation, and verify zero text inspection in core.
2. **`τ_err` / `θ_err_cos` & vectorless sensor sweep:** Sweep `τ_err ∈ [0.55, 0.88]` on synthetic labeled pairs of same-trap vs distinct-trap error traces (both chat embeddings and vectorless numeric-sensor traces) measuring precision/recall of cluster assignment (`F1`) and downstream error reduction.
3. **Cross-place workflow + retry-pollution test (`procedure` ablation):** Execute multi-step goals spanning two places (`lab/spec.md` L154) where Episode 1 contains 2–3 dead-end rejected retries plus 1 recovery branch before goal completion. Measure: (a) deterministic stripping of dead-end retries vs keeping `on_fail` fallback edges, (b) `goal_success_rate` and `steps_to_goal` on unseen parameterized test instances (`skill_ablation` vs single-place episode slicing vs `procedure_extraction = off`).
4. **Drift / poison-decoy & budget-pressure test (`2609.33013` protocol):** Plant a rule/procedure that succeeds `3–4` times and then flips (`environment drift` / `poison decoy`), plus run under memory budget pressure (`N` items > `max_items`). Verify: (a) cited failures drive `ρ_p < theta_proc_retire` and trigger `revise` or soft `retired` status (`superseded`), restoring task success to pre-drift levels; (b) budget pressure does not randomly evict high-credence load-bearing procedures (`transfer_retention >= baseline`).

## Established / inferred / open

- **Established:**
  - Contrastive learning comparing successful vs failed trajectories significantly outperforms failure-only buffering or naive appending (`ExpeL` `59.0% vs 40.3%`; `Mem^p` Adjustment `+0.7` & `-14` steps over Validation/Vanilla; `MACLA` `78.1%` avg / `90.3%` ALFWorld unseen via contrastive refinement + Beta posteriors + `θ_dup = 0.85`).
  - Parameterizing concrete literals into slots while filtering invalid/redundant retries improves task transfer and cuts steps (`AWM` `+51.1%` rel. WebArena, steps `7.9 -> 5.9`; `Mem^p Proceduralization` `77.86 vs 56.43` Script, steps `23.76 -> 15.01`).
  - Naively evicting memory under budget pressure destroys load-bearing items (`0.319` forgetting precision, `-15.0 pp` regression), whereas instability gating, reversibility, and provenance ledgers yield `0` admitted poison rules and `1.0` rollback recovery (`Annapureddy & Thamatani, 2026 [PP]`).
- **Inferred:**
  - Exact core-side error matching via `(error_kind, expect_kind, cap_id)` + embedding/magnitude affinity + contrast bundling implements `R-22` cleanly without ever violating the opaque-core / zero-content-reading invariant.
  - Cross-episode backward tracing along `goal_id`/`caused_by` edges solves the multi-place procedure extraction failure implied by `sleep-consolidation/spec.md` L25 vs `lab/spec.md` L154.
  - Warning/lesson credence updates symmetrically on `(avoided_repeat_error -> α+1, repeated_same_error_despite_citation -> β+1)`.
- **Open:**
  - **Not settled by the literature:** Exact optimal thresholds `τ_err = 0.72` and `θ_err_cos = 0.68` for deciding when two content-blind error events constitute the "same prediction error" under `EmbeddingGemma-300M` (or vectorless sensor traces); settle via Lab `τ_err` sweep.
  - **Not settled by the literature:** In chat-only vs embodied bodies, whether keeping `on_fail` fallback branches inline inside a single procedure helps more than extracting a standalone recovery sub-procedure when an error branch is hit; settle via Lab procedure-structure ablation.
