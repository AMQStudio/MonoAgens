## R-68 — Implicit Conversational Repair Detection (`p_user_correction`) & Long-Term Preference Drift

*(Full 20-paper matrix, OSI dataset audit, and equations in [`01_repair_detection_and_preference_drift.md`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/research/findings/run-7-notes/01_repair_detection_and_preference_drift.md).)*

### 1. Key Verified Literature (2025 – November 2026)
- **Conversational Repair & Implicit Feedback:**
  - `Reading Between the Turns` (**LUHME @ ACL 2026**, `2026.luhme-1.3` `[PR]`, Oct 2026): Raw repair is a weak standalone signal; precision (`PR-AUC`) jumps sharply when combining **rephrasing/repetition similarity**, assistant quality, and multi-turn context.
  - `Loci` (MIT & IBM, `arXiv:2610.15154` `[PP]`, Oct 2026): Probabilistic error localization cuts multi-turn recovery cost by **46–79%**.
  - `Know-It-All GPT vs Second-Guesser Claude` (**ACL 2026**, `arXiv:2604.19245` `[PR]`): Uncalibrated models polarize into ignoring valid corrections ("know-it-all") vs flipping prematurely ("second-guesser").
  - `GAVA` (`arXiv:2610.00282` `[PP]`, Oct 2026): Grounded expected-loss arbitration (`accept / reject / inspect / ask`) hits **98.7–100%** correction accuracy.
  - `ImplicitRM` (`arXiv:2603.23184` `[PP]`, Mar/Oct 2026) & `Implicit Feedback` (`arXiv:2507.23158` `[PP]`, `MIT` repo): Implicit feedback appears in **>50%** of later turns, but naive binary updates degrade quality; propensity-weighted continuous signals improve $R^2$ by **+57.7% to +63.2%**.
  - `Found in Conversation` (**NeurIPS 2026**, `arXiv:2605.24432` `[PR]`): Closes **$\ge$92%** of the ~39% multi-turn collapse via early clarification/repair.
- **Long-Term Preference Drift & Invariant Protection:**
  - `CAPTURE` (`arXiv:2609.02265` `[PP]`, Sep 2026): Multi-timescale POMDP ledger + uncertainty-triggered clarification reaches **71.5%** win rate while holding poisoning to **11.5%**.
  - `CORE` (**EMNLP 2026 Findings**, `arXiv:2609.12373` `[PR]`, Sep 2026): Uncertainty-gated belief revision isolates turn-local noise from persistent user preferences.
  - `MEMDRIFT` (`arXiv:2605.24941` `[PP]`, rev. Oct 2026): Chat style/personality preferences silently leak into tool parameters across 7 frontier models, spiking degradation from **0.05–0.12 $\to$ 0.38–0.83**—proving strict `DomainId` scoping is mandatory.
  - `MEMOIR` (`arXiv:2611.07631` `[PP]`, **Nov 2026**, `CC-BY-4.0`, **+11.92 pp** Recall@5), `PerMemBench` (`arXiv:2605.25535` `[PP]`, `CC-BY-4.0`), `VARS` (`arXiv:2603.20939` `[PP]`, `Apache-2.0`, **+3.2%**), `PAMU` (`arXiv:2510.09720` `[PP]`, style consistency **37% $\to$ 92%**), `PersonaMem-v2` (`arXiv:2512.06688` `[PP]`, `CC-BY-4.0`, **16×** fewer tokens), and `NS-DPO` (**ICML 2025**, `arXiv:2407.18676` `[PR]`).

### 2. Language-Agnostic Design for MonoAgens V2.2
- **Closing `h_t` Blind Spot (`C-9`):** Step `place_memory` (`h_t`) or cache `last_soul_act_emb` (post-`VisibleScope`) when Soul acts so `Gated DeltaNet-2` sees its own reply.
- **`p_user_correction` Head:** Compare minimal linear head (`+129` params) vs recommended **Directional Pairwise Subspace Head** (`~8.2K` params over `[h_mean, e_proj_user * e_proj_soul, |e_proj_user - e_proj_soul|]` — preserving negation/polarity subspaces that scalar cosine collapses!).
- **Dual Loops:** (A) High calibrated `p_user_correction` wakes Reasoning with `[last_soul_event_id, prior_user_event_id]` for selective `ThoughtAct::Revise` / `Taint`-gated clarification (`Loci`/`GAVA`); (B) emits event-sourced propensity-weighted delayed negative `AdaptFeedback` on `GateArm::Go` (fixing `C-10`).
- **Three-Timescale Preference Drift (`C-15`):**
  1. *Fast (hours–7 days, e.g., exam crunch):* Time-discounted `PlaceAdaptState` (`last_updated_ms`, `adapt_daily_lambda ≈ 0.85`) + fast `BetaCredence` shadow (`lambda_fast = 0.80/day`).
  2. *Medium (weeks–months):* Episode-gated slow `BeliefBook` (`0.98/day`) & `conventions` (`0.95/day`) promoted only after $\ge 2$ corroborating episodes (`PerMemBench`/`CORE`), strictly keyed by `(DomainId, PlaceId, Audience)` (`MEMDRIFT` guard) and exempted from Petrov fade (`fade.rs`).
  3. *Immutable Core ($\lambda = 1.0$):* `OwnerSigned` `IdentityAnchor`/`principles` and `Valve` rules never decay or accept chat-turn preference drift.

## R-69 — Per-Participant Working State (`h_actor`) & Interleaved Multi-Topic Group Chat Disentanglement

### 1. Key Verified Literature (2025 – November 2026)
- **Disentanglement:** `APO-DD` / `DD-GEPA` (`arXiv:2606.07894`, Oct 2026 `[PP]`, Ubuntu IRC NMI **95.51** / S-F1 **84.80**), `Pal et al.` (**LREC May 2026**, `2026.lrec-1.229` `[PR]` — zero-shot LLMs fail; fine-tuned GPT-4o Mini hits **74.4%** link F1 / **45.3%** conv F1, still behind compact structure-aware models), `Takada & Mori` (**LaCATODA @ AAAI Jan 2026** `[PR]`, `DLA+SC`), and `CluCDD` (`arXiv:2302.08146` `[PR]`).
- **Factorized Multi-Party State:**
  - `SinkState` (`arXiv:2611.14246` `[PP]`, **Nov 2026**): Proves linear-attention / Gated DeltaNet recurrent states are naturally low-rank (up to **2.6×** memory cut with zero accuracy loss) — justifying compact **`16d–32d` `h_actor`**!
  - `PERSIST` (`arXiv:2610.07725` `[PP]`, Oct 2026): **3W (Who-What-When)** joint scoring cuts retrieval latency from `>500 ms` to **7.03 ms** at **85.08%** *SpokenTrace* accuracy.
  - `SpeakerMem-R1` (`arXiv:2609.26780` `[PP]`, Sep 2026): Dual-track **group vs person** memory lifts writer QA **57.38% $\to$ 68.20%** (**69.2%** SocialMemBench, **70.85%** LoCoMo).
  - `GroupMemBench` (**NeurIPS 2026**, `arXiv:2605.14498` `[PR]`, `CC-BY-4.0 AND MIT`): Top memory systems collapse to **46.0%** avg (**27.1%** knowledge updates); `EverMemBench` (**KDD 2026 Oral**, `arXiv:2602.01313` `[PR]`, `CC-BY-4.0`/`Apache-2.0`): multi-hop drops to **26%**; `SocialMemBench` (`arXiv:2605.17789` `[PP]`): diagnoses **single-stream conflation**; `Inoue et al.` (IWSDS 2025 `[PR]`): only **~20%** of multi-party turns have explicit addressees.

### 2. Two-Stage Design (+0 Net Primitives, Strict $\Delta_{\text{NI}} = 0$)
1. **Stage 1 — Streaming 3W Multi-Slot Disentanglement inside `EpisodeBook` (`< 10 µs`, zero LLM calls):**
   - Replace single `active_by_place` slot (`episodes.rs:89`) with `M <= 6` active thread slots keyed by **`(PlaceId, CanonicalAudienceHash)`**, scored by `w_sem * cos(e_256, ep.centroid) + w_who * actor_overlap + w_when * exp(-Δt/τ)` (with `reply_to`/`caused_by` fast-path and `EventId` tie-breaking).
   - Store `thread_id` on `PlaceHistoryEntry`/`MemoryEntry` and prioritize trigger-thread siblings in `[PLACE_TAIL]` (`context_emit.rs` + `render.rs`).
   - **Fix `C-8` immediately:** Clear/recompute `EpisodeRecord.centroid` in `EpisodeBook::forget` whenever any event in a surviving partial episode is erased.
2. **Stage 2 — Factorized Working State `(h_place: 64d, h_actor: 24d–32d)`:**
   - First fix `state/mod.rs:720, 1747` so actor exchange stats work in group chats (`audience.len() > 2`).
   - **Critical Privacy Rule:** Key `h_actor` strictly by **`(PlaceId, AudienceHash, ActorId)`** (NEVER globally by `ActorId` — sharing across DM and group breaks $\Delta_{\text{NI}} = 0$!). Cap at `K_ACTORS = 32` per room (`~4 KiB` RAM, `+2–5 µs` CPU) with deterministic `(last_active_ms, ActorId)` LRU eviction in `BTreeMap`, and wipe cleanly on `EventKind::HardForget`. Ablate numeric `x_num` actor+thread features first vs full recurrent `h_actor` cell.

### 2. Layer-Clean Design (Zero New Core Primitives)
1. **Protocol / Edge (Additive `#[serde(default)]`):** Edge-attested `automated_peer: bool` on `ActorFacts` (platform bot badge / A2A `AgentCard`, never NL claims), `peer_expertise_margin: Option<f32>` on `Percept` computed via Gateway's existing `EmbeddingGemma 2` `256d` `Tool RAG`, plus `hop_count` / `visited_agents` cycle guard in `plugin-a2a-bridge`.
2. **Valve (`crates/valve`):** Deterministic `PeerLoopBrake` (`max_consecutive_automated_turns = 4` per `place`, safely below the 7-turn `Echoing` threshold, resetting to `0` on any non-automated turn) + `max_a2a_hops = 3`.
3. **Core (`crates/core`):** Gate `CommitmentInputs::turn_allocated` bypass when an automated peer repeatedly addresses Soul after `consecutive_automated_turns >= 2`; add deterministic `SeededRng` per-soul jitter scaled by `(1 + 2 * peer_expertise_margin)` to `OpenWait.due_ms` so faster/specialist bots speak first and others cleanly `Yielded` (zero chorus flooding!).
