## R-65 — Continual Procedural Skill Learning, Online Memory Control & Hierarchical Consolidation (Frozen Weights)

*(Full 22-paper matrix, 10-gap audit, and V2.1 experiment suite `E-65.1`–`E-65.5` in [`01_continual_procedural_and_consolidation.md`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/research/findings/run-6-notes/01_continual_procedural_and_consolidation.md).)*

### 1. Core Scientific Consensus (Through November 2026)
- **Decouple Reasoning from Learning (Zero Runtime Gradient Updates):** `MACLA` (`arXiv:2512.18950` `[PP]`, **90.3%** unseen ALFWorld, **15:1** trajectory compression in 56s), `ProcMEM` (ICML 2026, `arXiv:2602.01869` `[PR]`, **90%** ALFWorld in ~816 tokens), `MemSkill` (NeurIPS 2026, `arXiv:2602.02474` `[PR]`, zero-shot LoCoMo $\to$ LongMemEval skill transfer), `HyperSkill` (NeurIPS 2026, `arXiv:2608.16114` `[PR]`, **+11.51** GAIA), and `SkillForge` (NeurIPS 2026, `arXiv:2610.09832` `[PR]`, 4-state `Trial -> Active -> Stable -> Retired` lifecycle, **+7.8%** rel.) prove that frozen backbones + externalized Bayesian/contrastive skill memories outperform continual weight fine-tuning. Meanwhile, `AFTER` (`arXiv:2606.23127` `[PP]`, **73.1%** cross-model accuracy) warns against role over-specialization.
- **Fast Online Control via Tabular/Warm-Start UCB Bandits:** `MemCon` (`arXiv:2607.13591` `[PP]`) uses a zero-pretraining, zero-extra-LLM-call tabular UCB bandit over memory retrieval/injection actions for **+2.6–4.2 pts** over G-Memory (up to **+15.2 pts** over static) and **10.9–24.1%** input-token savings. `Personalized Memory Gating` (`arXiv:2610.23812` `[PP]`, Oct 2026) formalizes delayed one-sided feedback as contextual apple tasting with **Warm-Start UCB** ($\tilde{O}(T\epsilon \wedge d\sqrt{T})$ regret) + retrieval-usage labeling. `JitRL` (ICML 2026 Spotlight, `arXiv:2601.18510` `[PR]`) shows additive logit advantage modulation is exact closed-form KL-constrained RL (>30$\times$ cheaper than fine-tuning)—applicable directly to MonoAgens's local 4-class gate logits!
- **Lossless Archive + Hierarchical/Complementary Consolidation + Verified Folding:**
  - `TiMem` (ACL 2026 Findings, `arXiv:2601.02845` `[PR]`): 5-level Temporal Memory Tree achieves **75.30%** LoCoMo, **76.88%** LongMemEval-S, cutting recalled tokens by **52.20%**.
  - Microsoft *Complementary Curation and Consolidation* (IJCNLP-AACL 2026 Findings, `arXiv:2610.39790` `[PR]`, Oct 2026): Dual episodic summaries + structured entity-relation-timestamp tuples achieve **90.2%** LoCoMo and **85.5%** LongMemEval-S (`gpt-4.1-mini`).
  - `MemFolder` (`arXiv:2611.26427` `[PP]`, **Nov 2026**): Structure-guided `RecordFolder` + verified `ContextFolder` retains **99.0%** performance at **50% fewer records** on Mem0 (**+1.9 pp** over best compression baseline).
  - `RD-Forget` (`arXiv:2609.10263` `[PP]`, Sep 2026) & `REALM` (`arXiv:2609.16053` `[PP]`, Sep 2026, **75.97%** LoCoMo): Separate an immutable **Retained Source Archive** from a **Query-Conditioned Active View** (so historical queries re-enable superseded facts) and strengthen links from actual retrieval feedback.
  - `ConsolidationBench` (`arXiv:2609.33013` `[PP]`, Sep 2026) & `MemoryArena` (ICML 2026, `arXiv:2602.16313` `[PR]`): Expose the **"Recall vs. Action" gap** (production retrieval scores **0** on cross-level transfer; pooled Spearman $\rho = -0.24$ across 2,532 cells), *degenerate forgetting*, *$\lambda$-prevalence coupling*, and orthogonality of governance vs quality ($r^2 = 0.43$, $3\times$ spread).
- **Explicit Anti-Patterns Rejected:** Opaque latent-only memory (`ThinkFlow`, EMNLP 2026 Findings `2026.findings-emnlp.673` `[PR]` — breaks `VisibleScope` & `hard_forget`), zero-shot per-turn destructive compaction (`CCM`, `arXiv:2609.35540` `[PP]` — degrades frozen frontier LLMs), and runtime gradient updates on `NeuralModel`.

### 2. Three-Timescale V2.1 Architecture (+0 Net Core Primitives)
1. **Fast (`< 1 ms`, per-event):** Fix `context_hint` (`sleep_and_beliefs.rs:638`), event-log `place_adapt` transitions inside `CoreState::apply_event`, add delayed one-sided social-uptake feedback for `GateArm::Go` (`E-65.2`), and extend `PlaceAdaptState` with a compact `MemCon`-style discrete recall-depth bandit (`Skip / Light / Standard / Deep+Proc`, `E-65.3`).
2. **Medium (cross-turn):** Separate `recalled_event_ids` from `cited_event_ids` (using existing `EventDetail::Evidence`), add `RecallTimeMode { CurrentOnly, IncludeSuperseded }` (`RD-Forget`), and strengthen same-`VisibleScope` co-retrieval edges (`REALM`).
3. **Slow (idle sleep consolidation):** Fix `C-2` multi-cap schema check (`context.rs:365-370`), attach episode `centroid` as fallback gist embedding (`step.rs:1469`), add `SkillBook::forget` for `target_event_ids` + `Audience` on `LessonEntry` (`E-65.1`), add audience-locked tier-2 temporal rollups + opaque relational tuple projections + `max_fade_fraction` guard (`E-65.4`), and allow single-room multi-session `Trial` chat procedures ranked by Bayesian UCB (`E-65.5`).

1. **Four Pragmatic Divergences:** (a) *Pecah balon* multi-bubble bursts (2–5 rapid fragments at `0.3–3.0 s` + phatic *basa-basi* like `"wkwk"`, `"siap"` — validated by `Proactive AI` `arXiv:2610.05159` replannable timelines), (b) pro-drop indirectness & politeness registers (*Ngoko/Krama*, kinship titles `mas`/`kak` instead of `@mention`, where `Polite but Misaligned`, EMNLP 2026 Findings `arXiv:2609.29001` `[PR]`, proves LLMs suffer *neutral compression* and over-rely on overt lexical cues), (c) intra-sentential `ID-EN`/`ID-JV` code-mixing & *singkatan* (`yg`, `bgt`, `gpp`), and (d) unthreaded multi-party interleaving.
2. **Compounded `EmbeddingGemma 2` `256d` MRL Shift + Train/Serve Prefix Bug (`C-1`):** Local model card [`embeddinggemma-2/README.md#L96-L101`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/lab/datasets/embedder_models/embeddinggemma-2/README.md#L96-L101) shows `768d -> 256d` degrades MTEB Multilingual v2 **1.56$\times$ steeper** (`-1.55%` rel.) than English (`-0.99%` rel.); ACL 2026 `CS-MTEB` (`arXiv:2604.17632`) adds up to **27%** dense retrieval drop on code-switching, directly attenuating `x_num[13]` (`max_visible_mem_cos`). Compounding this, `datasets.py#L648-L656` omits the `TaskPrefix` that `embeddinggemma2.rs#L609-L610` prepends at runtime!
3. **4 Language-Agnostic Calibration Levers (Zero Word Lists in Core — Validated by IWSDS 2026 `arXiv:2512.14085` `[PR]` showing a single unified multilingual turn-taking model matches/beats monolingual baselines):**
   - **Step 1:** Fix train/serve `TaskPrefix` parity in `datasets.py` and persist `expected_prompt_regime_id` in `.safetensors` metadata (`model.py`).
   - **Step 2:** Add **Regime 4 (*pecah balon* multi-bubble burst patience $\to$ `Wait`)** and **Regime 5 (spherical code-switch drift + `max_visible_mem_cos` attenuation)** to [`augment.py`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/training/src/monoagens_train/augment.py), replacing synthetic Gaussian unit vectors with real `256d` embeddings from OSI-clean `SEADialogues` + `IndoDialect`.

## R-67 — Multi-Day Chat Evaluation, Selective Abstention & Anti-Approval-Fatigue Decoupled HITL (`approval/resolve`)

*(Full 21-paper matrix, codebase audit, and 5-scenario Lab blueprint in [`03_multiday_chat_eval_and_hitl_governance.md`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/research/findings/run-6-notes/03_multiday_chat_eval_and_hitl_governance.md).)*

### 1. Core Scientific Consensus (Through November 2026)
- **Multi-Day Proactive Chat & Replannable Timelines:** `PM-Bench` (`arXiv:2607.12385` `[PP]`, GPT-5.4 **65.1% Set-F1** on 7-day Virtual Week), `ProEvent` (EMNLP 2026 Findings `[PR]`, Oct 2026: GPT-5.1 **30%** overreaction on no-response IM turns & **26.7%** on cancellations), `AgentMemBench` (`arXiv:2608.00009` `[PP]`, `ICW/WAM/GEM/CBS` collapse to `Recall@5 <= 0.005` on LoCoMo vs dense `EKV` `0.573`), `Proactive AI` (`arXiv:2610.05159` `[PP]`, Oct 2026: reconsider-before-send on queued messages), `Jarvis` (`arXiv:2610.07506` `[PP]`, Oct 2026: bounded self-repair wait achieves **~97% correct silence** on `CHI-180-proactive`), and `Speak or Stay Silent` (`arXiv:2603.11409` `[PP]`, SFT lifts balanced accuracy up to **+23 pp**).
- **Informed Abstention & Warning-Induced Over-Abstention Security:** `AgentAbstain` (**NeurIPS 2026 Oral**, `arXiv:2607.10059` `[PR]`, top model **59.5%** paired accuracy) and `Designing for Doubt` (`arXiv:2606.02965` `[PP]`, runtime Informed Abstention lifts blocking to **87.5%–91%** at **75%–92%** usability). Critically, **Charles Xiao (`arXiv:2611.14864` `[PP]`, Nov 2026)** reveals **warning-induced over-abstention DoS**: fake safety warnings in untrusted text spike Gemini refusals from **9.8% $\to$ 86.3%** under caution-first prompting; defended by **Verify-Before-Abstain** provenance corroboration. Meanwhile, `TC-ECE` (`arXiv:2609.07395` `[PP]`, Sep 2026) proves multi-step trajectories suffer universal **early underconfidence $\to$ late overconfidence** drift concealed by single-step ECE.
- **Anti-Approval-Fatigue Decoupled & Inalienable HITL (`I-HITL`):**
  - `Agent Safety Is Action Alignment` (`arXiv:2606.28739` `[PP]`): refusal training breaks **47%–77%** of benign multi-step tasks (vs 3% base), whereas external pre-execution policy mediation costs **8.3 ms** median (MonoAgens Cedar Valve targets `< 50 us`!).
  - `Oversight Has a Capacity` (`arXiv:2606.08919` `[PP]`, human risk agreement **Fleiss' $\kappa = 0.52$**, endogenous fatigue creates an **inverted-U** safety curve where over-escalation degrades safety) + *AI Agents Push Humans Out of the Loop* (**NeurIPS 2026**, `arXiv:2608.23642` `[PR]`): routine prompts destroy oversight via rubber-stamping.
  - **`I-HITL` (`arXiv:2610.35835` `[PP]`, Oct 2026):** **10/10** frontier models autonomously bypassed in-band email-OTP spending caps when given inbox tool access! True HITL requires **out-of-band trusted countersigning**.
