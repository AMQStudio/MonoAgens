## 2. Deep Analysis: Indonesian/SEA Group-Chat Pragmatics & `EmbeddingGemma 2` `256d` Geometry

### 2.1 Four Indonesian & SEA Group-Chat Phenomena That Break English-Trained Controllers

MonoAgens's current Phase 1–3 controller (`1M + Gated DeltaNet-2`, trained on English `when2speak`, `ubuntu_irc`, `proactive_bench`, `capa`, `longmemeval`, `group_mem_bench`, `stale`, `hi_tom`, `dyntom`) operates strictly on `e_256` (`256d` frozen embedding) + `x_num` (`18d` numeric vector in [`crates/core/src/neural/features.rs`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/neural/features.rs#L82-L108)) + per-place `64d` `h_t`. In Indonesian and SEA messaging (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, Slack), four structural dynamics diverge sharply from English training corpora:

1. **Message Fragmentation (*Pecah Balon*) & Phatic *Basa-Basi*:**
   - Indonesian chat participants routinely split a single communicative turn into 2–5 rapid bubbles (`"eh"`, `"bentar"`, `"yg td jadinya gmn"`, `"wkwk"`, `"siap"`, `"gas"`, `"otw"`) separated by `0.3–3.0 s`.
   - Under MonoAgens's current `when2speak` / `ubuntu_irc` training distribution, rapid bursts without explicit `Wait` supervision trigger premature `Go` false-speaks (already visible in the offline test drive on `dev-07-habituation` and `dev-08-patience`, [`2026-11-21-v2-offline-test-drive.md`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/research/findings/2026-11-21-v2-offline-test-drive.md#L65-L66)).
   - Phatic acknowledgments (*basa-basi*: laughter, agreement tokens, sticker/emoji reactions) are **non-floor-yielding** social grease — they should yield `Silent` or `Note`, never `Go`. Consistent with **Proactive AI** (`arXiv:2610.05159`), pending responses must remain replannable across bursts rather than firing on fragment #1.
2. **Pro-Drop Indirectness, Politeness Registers & Kinship Addressees:**
   - Indonesian and regional languages (`Javanese` *Ngoko*/*Krama*, `Sundanese` *Loma*/*Lemes*, `Minangkabau`) routinely drop subjects/pronouns, signal social distance through register shifts (`LoraxBench`, `arXiv:2508.12459`), and address participants via informal kinship/role titles (`mas`, `mbak`, `kak`, `bang`, `min`) rather than platform `@mention` tags.
   - Consequently, `x_num[0]` (`is_named_soul`) and `x_num[1]` (`is_explicit_addressee`) are frequently `0.0` even when a human expects a turn — shifting burden onto `e_256` + `h_t` + `x_num[2]` (`is_reply_or_expect_link`) + `x_num[6..9]` (cadence/talk-share).
   - Meanwhile, **`Polite but Misaligned`** (`arXiv:2609.29001`, EMNLP 2026 Findings) demonstrates that LLMs over-rely on overt lexical politeness cues and suffer *neutral compression* on implicit rapport/face-work — meaning hard-coded lexical rules would systematically fail, and pragmatic nuance must be handled jointly by calibrated `Wait`/restraint in Core and culturally grounded Reasoning in the LLM.
3. **Intra-Sentential Code-Mixing (`ID-EN` *Bahasa Jaksel*, `ID-JV`, `ID-SU`) & *Singkatan* Orthography:**
   - Urban Indonesian group chat blends English nouns/verbs/discourse markers into Indonesian syntax (`"literally gue lagi burnout bgt sih, btw deploy-nya jd di-hold gak?"`) alongside vowel deletion (`yg`, `tdk`, `bgt`, `gpp`, `blm`) and regional suffixes (`-e`, `-ne`, `-mah`, `-atuh`, `-lah`).
   - Crucially, **both `IndoSafety` (`arXiv:2506.02573`) and `PingPong` (`arXiv:2601.17277`)** show that models trained on formal/monolingual data degrade disproportionately on colloquial and code-switched multi-turn threads.
4. **Unthreaded Multi-Party Interleaving:**
   - Casual group chats answer open questions 2–5 messages later *without* invoking platform reply-threading (`percept.reply_to == None`). If the controller fails to enter `Wait` (`wait_delta_ms ∈ [2000, 6000] ms`) when an open-floor question lands, it barges over human teammates (`dev-08-patience` failure mode).

   - **ACL 2026 `CS-MTEB`** (`arXiv:2604.17632`) establishes that code-switched queries diverge geometrically in dense embedding space from their monolingual counterparts (up to **27% retrieval drop**). Because MRL concentrates coarse cross-lingual topical axes in early dimensions and fine lexical/morphological distinctions in higher dimensions, slicing to `256d` on colloquial Indonesian or `ID-EN` code-mixing risks attenuating cosine similarity between a colloquial incoming message (`"gmn deploy td?"`) and a stored formal/English memory (`"Deployment status update"`).
   - Inside [`crates/core/src/neural/features.rs`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/neural/features.rs#L258-L282), that cosine drop directly lowers **`x_num[13]` (`max_visible_mem_cos`)** (computed strictly post-`VisibleScope` via [`cosine_similarity`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/neural/features.rs#L54-L77)), silently biasing `gate_4` and `p_memory_relevance` downward on colloquial/code-mixed turns!
2. **CRITICAL CODEBASE MISMATCH — Unprefixed Training Shards vs Task-Prefixed Runtime Embeddings:**
   - At runtime, [`crates/reason/src/embed/embeddinggemma2.rs`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/reason/src/embed/embeddinggemma2.rs#L609-L610) calls `input.task.format_text(text)`, prepending the canonical English instruction prefix from [`crates/reason/src/embed/mod.rs`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/reason/src/embed/mod.rs#L58-L65):
     - `TaskPrefix::Clf` -> `"task: classification | query: "`
     - `TaskPrefix::Sts` -> `"task: sentence similarity | query: "`
     - `TaskPrefix::Query` -> `"task: search result | query: "`
     - `TaskPrefix::Document` -> `"title: none | text: "`
   - Conversely, offline training shard generation in [`training/src/monoagens_train/datasets.py`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/training/src/monoagens_train/datasets.py#L648-L656) calls `st_model.encode([{"text": s} for s in ordered_texts], truncate_dim=256, normalize_embeddings=True)` **without `prompt_name` and without prepending any task prefix**!
   - Google's own model card ([`README.md`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/lab/datasets/embedder_models/embeddinggemma-2/README.md#L130-L133)) explicitly warns that omitting task prefixes alters embedding geometry and reduces precision. Worse, on 2–4 token colloquial Indonesian messages (`"otw kak"`, `"gas bgt"`), an English prefix (`"task: classification | query: "`) dominates the mean-pooled token window (`~6` English prefix tokens vs `2–4` Indonesian tokens), shifting the resulting `256d` unit vector away from the unprefixed manifold on which [`MonoNeuralController.emb_linear`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/training/src/monoagens_train/model.py#L138-L139) was trained.
3. **English-Only QA Normalization in Lab Scoring:**
   - [`crates/lab/src/datasets/scoring_utils.rs`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/lab/src/datasets/scoring_utils.rs#L59-L112) (`normalize_qa_tokens` + `porter_stem_light`) strips ASCII-only English articles (`"a"|"an"|"the"|"and"`) and applies English suffix stemming (`-ing`, `-ed`, `-ly`).
