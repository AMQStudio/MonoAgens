## 2. Deep Technical Synthesis

### 2.1 Self-Echo (`Agency M20`) vs. Inter-Agent Ping-Pong vs. Multi-Bot Chorus Flooding

MonoAgens already solves **self-echo** (`Agency M20`), but **self-echo**, **inter-agent ping-pong**, **chorus flooding**, and **integrative compromise** are distinct failure modes requiring different invariants:

| Failure Mode | Trigger Topology | Why `Agency M20` Does / Does Not Catch It | Empirical Consequence (2025–2026 Literature) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Self-Echo (`M20`)** *(Solved)* | `Soul` emits action $\to$ platform reflects own message or physical sensor effect back to `Soul`. | **Caught:** `ExpectationBook::match_self_caused_effect` matches `correlation_id`, `percept.actor == soul_actor_id`, or physical sensor `(channel, [lat_min, lat_max], |\Delta_{obs} - \Delta_{pred}| \le k\sigma)` and short-circuits appraisal (`DecisionReason::SelfCaused`). | Prevented completely in MonoAgens (`crates/core/src/expectations/mod.rs:708-780`). |
| **Inter-Agent Ping-Pong** *(Unsolved)* | `Soul` speaks in `room:group` $\to$ `Bot_B` replies (or `@soul` / `deg_soul >= θ`) $\to$ `Soul` wakes and replies $\to$ `Bot_B` replies $\to \dots$ with **no human speaking in between**. | **Not caught:** `percept.actor = bot_b != soul_id` and `correlation_id` differs. If `Bot_B` omits `reply_to`/`caused_by` (flat room stream), even `Valve`'s `ChainBrake` (`chain_wake_counts`) does not trigger. | Unbounded token/cost exhaustion (`IAL`, `arXiv:2607.01641`) and **32.8%–70%** persona echoing drift after **7+ turns** (`arXiv:2511.09710`). |
| **Multi-Bot Chorus Flooding** *(Unsolved)* | Human `Alice` asks one open question in `room:group` containing `[Alice, Soul, Bot_B, Bot_C]`. | **Partially mitigated, fragile:** `OpenWait` + `Yielded` only cancel if another bot answers *before* `due_ms` expires. With `wait_delta_ms = 0` or identical un-jittered timers across homogeneous instances, all $N$ bots wake at the same `tick` and emit $N$ redundant answers simultaneously. | Redundant "social sycophancy" / chorus replies (`MASCOT`, `arXiv:2601.14230`) and near-random open-floor turn-taking in multi-party agents (`MP-Bench`, `arXiv:2609.13076`). |
| **Expertise Neglect / Integrative Compromise** *(Unsolved)* | `Alice` asks a specialist question (e.g., SQL migration) in a room with a generalist `Soul` and a specialist `Bot_DB`. | **Not caught:** `Core` has no awareness of peer capabilities vs. own capabilities (`Tool RAG` indexes *own* capabilities only), so `Soul` may speak anyway and dilute `Bot_DB`'s answer. | Teams drop up to **37.6%–41.1%** below their single best expert due to averaging expert & non-expert outputs (`arXiv:2602.01011`). |

### 2.2 Anti-Ping-Pong Mechanisms

From `IAL-Scan` (`arXiv:2607.01641`), `Echoing` (`arXiv:2511.09710`), and `A2ABreak` (`arXiv:2609.10871`), infinite bot loops arise from three gaps:
1. **Unbroken Causal/Flat-Room Feedback Cycles:** Bots reply to each other on flat chat platforms (Matrix/Slack/Discord) where `reply_to` is omitted, defeating thread-local `ChainBrake` counters. A **per-place consecutive automated-turn counter** (`consecutive_peer_turns` since the last non-automated / human turn) bounds loops regardless of threading metadata.
2. **Commitment-Bypass Escalation by Peer Bots:** If `Bot_B` writes `@soul` in turn 6 of a bot-only exchange, hard `turn_allocated` bypass (`CommitmentInputs::turn_allocated`) forces a model wake even when the room has turned into a bot-only ping-pong loop. Automated actors must **lose** unconditional `Commitment` bypass once `consecutive_automated_turns` exceeds a small bound (e.g., `K_bot = 2` or `3`), handing control to the Neural Gate / Valve cooldown.
3. **Structured Termination & Cooldown:** When `consecutive_automated_turns >= max_bot_turns_without_human` (e.g., `max_bot_turns = 4`–`6`, strictly below the `~7+` turn onset of `Echoing` drift in `arXiv:2511.09710`), the **Valve** deterministically refuses or holds further autonomous wakes/speaks in that `place` until a non-automated actor speaks or an explicit owner/human re-engages.

### 2.3 Chorus Prevention & Decentralized Floor Etiquette

`BusMA` (`arXiv:2609.15054`) and `DeLM` (`arXiv:2606.10662`) show that centralized LLM routers (`SelectorGroupChat` / HMW) add single-point routing errors and latency bubbles, whereas `Cocktail-Talker` (`arXiv:2607.27756`) and `SpeakerTurn` (`arXiv:2610.39828`) demonstrate strong gains when agents treat **`Wait` / `Listen` / `Ignore`** as first-class actions rather than binary Speak/Silent:
- MonoAgens **already has** the 4-class gate `[Silent, Note, Wait, Go]` (`GateClass` + `OpenWait` + `DecisionReason::Yielded`)!
- To eliminate **simultaneous chorus flooding** without a central room-level LLM coordinator:
  1. **Peer-Presence Floor Sensitivity:** When a room contains ≥ 1 other automated peer (`peer_agent_count >= 1`) and the human did **not** explicitly address `Soul` (`!is_explicit_addressee && !is_named_soul`), the gate should bias toward `Wait` (`WaitDeferral`) rather than immediate `Go`.
  2. **Deterministic Per-Soul Jittered Backoff:** If two MonoAgens souls run the same weights in one room, identical `wait_delta_ms` outputs from `head.wait_delta_t` cause **thundering-herd wake collisions** at `tick`. Adding a deterministic per-soul, per-event seeded jitter derived from `(seed, soul_actor_id, event_id)` spreads `OpenWait.due_ms` across `[wait_delta_ms, wait_delta_ms + jitter_max_ms]` (e.g., `0..1500 ms`) so the first bot to speak cancels every other bot's `OpenWait` cleanly via `DecisionReason::Yielded`!
  3. **Peer-Aware `OpenWait` Cancellation:** Currently `OpenWait::matches_third_party_reply` cancels on *any* third party. In multi-bot rooms, if another peer bot answers the human's question first, `Soul` yields (`Yielded`) and updates `PlaceAdaptState` (`GateArm::Wait`, reward `0.5` or higher when chorus avoided) — preventing double-answering.

### 2.4 Expertise Deferral (Avoiding "Integrative Compromise")

`Multi-Agent Teams Hold Experts Back` (`arXiv:2602.01011`) proves that even if an agent knows another agent is the expert, **speaking anyway** degrades accuracy by up to **37.6%–41.1%** via compromise. Conversely, `ModeratorLM` (`arXiv:2606.13544`) shows role/expertise-conditioned turn-taking boosts precision by **>40%** and recall by **>70%**.
- **Design implication for MonoAgens:** Deferral must happen **pre-wake at the Gate**, not post-wake in the LLM prompt!
- At the Gateway edge (using the same `EmbeddingGemma 2` `256d` encoder already used for `Tool RAG` `k_cap = 8` in `design.md` D4), Gateway can compute a bounded scalar **`peer_expertise_margin`** (or `max_peer_cap_cos - soul_cap_cos`, mapped to `[0.0, 1.0]`) against registered peer `CapabilityDescriptor`s / A2A `AgentCard.skills` present in that `place`.
- When another peer in the room has higher domain capability similarity (or when a peer with higher domain match has already entered `Working` / spoken), `Soul` extends `wait_delta_ms` / selects `Wait` or `Silent` rather than chiming in with low-confidence generalist commentary.

### 2.5 Bounded `A2A` Task Delegation vs. Shared-Room Group Chat

`Predoaia et al.` (`arXiv:2607.23884`) and `A2ABreak` (`arXiv:2609.10871`) clarify why **bilateral `A2A` delegation** and **shared-room group chat** must stay separated at the protocol/place boundary:
- **Bilateral `A2A` (`a2a:<agent>`):** Point-to-point, task-scoped (`A2aTaskState`: `Submitted → Working → InputRequired → Completed/Failed/Canceled`), triggered deliberately by `Soul` emitting an `Action` (`a2a.<agent>.<skill>`), governed by `max_poll_attempts`, `PendingApproval`, and `ChainBrake` (`max_chain_wakes`) against multi-hop delegation loops (`A0 → A1 → A2 → A0`).
- **Shared-Room Group Chat (`room:<id>`):** Open multi-actor broadcast `Audience` with unsolicited `Percept`s, governed by floor etiquette (`OpenWait` + jitter + `Yielded`), peer-turn loop brakes, and expertise deferral.
- **Bridge rule:** Results returned from a private `a2a:<agent>` delegation carry `Taint::Untrusted` and private `Audience([soul, a2a:<agent>])` (`Line 2` privacy!), so `Soul` synthesizes the result before explicitly acting in `room:<id>`—preventing raw A2A chatter from leaking into or triggering group-room ping-pong.

## 5. Candidate Design, Multi-Bot Lab Scenario, & Unmeasured Items

### 5.1 Candidate Design (Minimal, Layer-Clean Extensions)

#### Layer 1 — Edge / Gateway (`crates/gateway`, `bodies/*`)
1. **Bot / Peer Provenance Translation:** Chat/voice/A2A adapters map platform-verified bot flags or A2A agent identities to `ActorFacts` (`automated_peer: true`, default `false` when unknown/unattested). Natural-language claims (`"I am a bot"`) never set this flag.
2. **Peer Capability Overlap (`EmbeddingGemma 2` `256d`):** When a room `place` has registered peer `CapabilityDescriptor`s (e.g., from `AgentCard.skills` or sibling body descriptors), Gateway reuses its existing `Tool RAG` embedder (`design.md` D4) to compute:
   $$\text{peer\_expertise\_margin} = \max_{c \in \text{PeerCaps}(place)} \cos(e_{256}, e_c) - \max_{c \in \text{SoulCaps}(place)} \cos(e_{256}, e_c)$$
   clamped to `[0.0, 1.0]` (default `None` / `0.0` in vectorless fallback).

#### Layer 2 — Protocol (`crates/protocol`, Additive `#[serde(default)]`)
1. **`ActorFacts` (`message.rs:303`):** Add `#[serde(default)] pub automated_peer: bool` (and optional `#[serde(default)] pub capability_hint_cos: Option<f32>` or keep capability overlap per-percept).
2. **`Percept` (`message.rs:125`):** Add `#[serde(default, skip_serializing_if = "Option::is_none")] pub peer_expertise_margin: Option<f32>`.
3. **`CapabilityDescriptor` / `A2a` `_meta`:** Track `hop_count: u8` and `visited_agents: Vec<ActorId>` in `plugin-a2a-bridge` metadata (`arXiv:2609.10871`) so cycles `A → B → A` fail closed with `ReceiptOutcome::Refused` (`ERR_A2A_CYCLE`).

#### Layer 3 — Valve (`crates/valve`, Deterministic Loop-Breaker)
1. **Per-Place Automated-Turn Brake (`valve.inbound.peer_loop_brake` & `valve.action.peer_loop_brake`):**
   - Extend `ValveSnapshot` (`snapshot.rs`) with `consecutive_automated_turns_by_place: BTreeMap<PlaceId, u32>` and `automated_actors: BTreeSet<ActorId>` derived purely from the event log: increments when `percept.actor ∈ automated_actors ∪ {soul_id}`, **resets to `0`** whenever any non-automated actor (`∉ automated_actors`) emits an admitted percept in that `place`.
   - New `ValveConfig` parameter `max_consecutive_automated_turns: u32` (suggested starting calibration: `4`, safely below the `7+` turn onset of `Echoing` drift in `arXiv:2511.09710`, and complementing `max_chain_wakes = 8` for unthreaded flat rooms).
   - When `consecutive_automated_turns >= max_consecutive_automated_turns` and the incoming percept (or outbound autonomous action) is from an automated actor without an intervening non-automated turn, Valve returns `DoorDecision::Refuse` (Inbound) or `DoorDecision::Hold` (Action) with a dedicated `ValveReasonTag::PeerLoopBrake` (`"peer_loop_brake"`).
2. **A2A Multi-Hop Cap:** `max_a2a_hops: u8` (default `3`) enforced deterministically before dispatching `a2a.*` actions.

#### Layer 4 — Core (`crates/core`, Reusing Existing Primitives Only)
1. **Scoped `CommitmentInputs` Guard (`state/mod.rs:505`):**
   - Do **not** trigger unconditional `CommitmentInputs::turn_allocated` bypass solely because an `automated_peer` actor `@mentioned` or oriented toward `soul_id` after `consecutive_automated_turns >= 2` in that `place`. Instead, route through `decide_gate` so the neural `[Silent, Note, Wait, Go]` policy and `CIC` abstention retain veto power.
2. **Seeded Per-Soul `OpenWait` Jitter (`decide.rs` / `step.rs:628`):**
   - When `gate.gate_class == GateClass::Wait` in a multi-participant room (`non_soul_audience >= 2`), compute deterministic jitter from `SeededRng::draw_unit(config.seed ^ 0x4A17_7E01, event_id)` scaled by `(1.0 + peer_expertise_margin)`:
     $$\text{effective\_wait\_ms} = \text{wait\_delta\_ms} + \lfloor u \cdot J_{\max} \cdot (1 + 2 \cdot \text{peer\_expertise\_margin}) \rfloor$$
     (e.g., $J_{\max} = 1500\text{ ms}$). Higher-expertise peers or faster-responding bots speak first, triggering `OpenWait::matches_third_party_reply` $\to$ `DecisionReason::Yielded` on slower bots, preventing chorus flooding and integrative compromise (`arXiv:2602.01011`).
3. **Versioned Neural Feature Extension (`feature_schema_version = 2`, `features.rs`):**
   - Extend `x_num` from `18d` to `22d` (keeping `v1` `18d` loader compatibility) with 4 bounded `[0, 1]` post-`VisibleScope` features:
     - `x[18]`: `is_sender_automated_peer` (`0.0` or `1.0` from `ActorFactsEntry`),
     - `x[19]`: `room_has_other_automated_peers` (`0.0` or `1.0`),
     - `x[20]`: `log1p_norm(consecutive_automated_turns_in_place, 16.0)`,
     - `x[21]`: `sanitize_unit(peer_expertise_margin, 0.0)`.
