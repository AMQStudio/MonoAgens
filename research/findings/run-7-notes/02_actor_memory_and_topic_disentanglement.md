## Executive Summary

1. **Busy Group Chats Expose Two Coupled Structural Gaps in MonoAgens V2:**
   - **Single-Stream Place Memory Conflation (`h_t`):** [`CoreState::place_memory`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/state/mod.rs#L115-L116) maintains one `64d` recurrent state (`minGRU` or `Gated DeltaNet-2`) per `PlaceId`, and [`CoreState::actor_exchanges`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/state/mod.rs#L714-L727) stops recording bilateral actor closeness whenever `recipients.len() > 3` (`DEFAULT_EXCHANGE_LISTENER_MAX = 3.0`) or `audience.len() > 2` ([`state/mod.rs#L1745-L1753`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/state/mod.rs#L1745-L1753)). In `GroupMemBench` (`arXiv:2605.14498`), `SocialMemBench` (`arXiv:2605.17789`), and `EverMemBench` (`arXiv:2602.01313`), conflating participants into a single room stream causes severe attribution collapse (top memory frameworks average **46.0%** on `GroupMemBench`, with **27.1%** on knowledge updates; multi-hop drops to **26%** on `EverMemBench` even with oracle evidence).
   - **Unthreaded Interleaving in `EpisodeBook` and `PLACE_TAIL`:** [`EpisodeBook::active_by_place`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/consolidation/episodes.rs#L89) tracks only **one** `Open` episode per `PlaceId` and segments solely by `missed_expectation` or time gap (`gap_ms > max(1_800_000, sleep_idle_ms)` at [`episodes.rs#L136-L140`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/consolidation/episodes.rs#L136-L140)), even though `EpisodeRecord` already maintains a normalized `256d` `centroid` ([`episodes.rs#L68, L429-L459`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/consolidation/episodes.rs#L68)). Similarly, [`context_emit.rs#L267-L310`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/turn/context_emit.rs#L267-L310) builds `[PLACE_TAIL]` (`PLACE_TAIL_LIMIT = 16`) as a flat chronological slice with zero thread grouping.
2. **2026 Literature Resolves *Where* Disentanglement & `h_actor` Belong in the Stack:**
   - **Disentanglement (`2025–2026`):** `Pal et al.` (LREC 2026 `[PR]`) and `Takada & Mori` (LaCATODA @ AAAI 2026 `[PR]`; `APO-DD` / `arXiv:2606.07894` `[PP]`) show that zero-shot LLMs perform poorly on thread disentanglement without specialized dialogue-level + look-ahead (`Subsequent Context`) prompts, which are too expensive and latency-bound for Core's pre-wake gate. Conversely, **online embedding-centroid + actor + $\Delta t$ streaming linking** extends [`EpisodeBook`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/consolidation/episodes.rs#L86-L92) directly with bounded `M <= 4..8` active thread slots per place (`O(M)` per percept, zero raw text, zero word lists), while `crates/reason/` handles optional semantic refinement during idle `ModelPurpose::Consolidate`.
   - **Factorized `h_actor` (`2025–Nov 2026`):** `SpeakerMem-R1` (`arXiv:2609.26780` `[PP]`), `PERSIST` (`arXiv:2610.07725` `[PP]`), and `LatentMem` (`arXiv:2602.03036`, EMNLP 2026 `[PR]`) demonstrate that separating **group/place state** from **per-speaker state** (and scoring jointly on Who-What-When) prevents cross-talk and persona bleeding. Meanwhile, `SinkState` (`arXiv:2611.14246` `[PP]`) shows linear-attention/Gated-DeltaNet recurrent states are naturally low-rank, supporting a compact **`16d–32d` `h_actor`** alongside `64d` `h_place`.
3. **Critical `VisibleScope` & `ARCHITECTURE.md` Findings:**
   - **Privacy Non-Interference ($\Delta_{\text{NI}} = 0$):** A global `h_actor[actor]` shared across rooms would leak private DM state into group-chat gate decisions. Therefore, `h_actor` **must** be keyed by `(PlaceId, AudienceDigest, ActorId)` (or strictly isolated per `(PlaceId, ActorId)` within matching `Audience`) and **never** cross audience boundaries.
   - **Complexity Budget & `HardForget`:** Extending `EpisodeBook` (`M` active thread slots per place) replaces single-slot `active_by_place` without adding a new deterministic primitive ([`ARCHITECTURE.md:268-271`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/ARCHITECTURE.md#L268-L271)). `h_actor` must be LRU-capped per place (deterministic `(last_active_ms, ActorId)` eviction) and bit-cleanly scrubbed in [`CoreState::apply_event`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/state/mod.rs#L1474-L1569) on `EventKind::HardForget`.

## 2. Deep Technical Synthesis

### 2.1 Online Streaming Disentanglement: Neural Edge vs. `crates/reason/` vs. `episodes.rs`

In busy group chats (Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, IRC), 2–4 topics routinely interleave within the same `PlaceId` over seconds or minutes, often with `percept.reply_to == None` (`~80%` of turns lack explicit addressees in multi-party dialogue, `arXiv:2501.16643`). Evaluating the three architectural placements against MonoAgens's two-speed loop ([`ARCHITECTURE.md:19-23, 75, 85-97`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/ARCHITECTURE.md#L19-L23)):

| Candidate Layer | How Disentanglement Works | Latency & Cost | Zero-Text / Privacy Compliance | Verdict for MonoAgens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. `crates/reason/` (Live LLM DLA/SC per percept)** | Prompt LLM with `[PLACE_TAIL]` + `DLA`/`SC` (`Takada & Mori`, `APO-DD`) to assign thread IDs. | **Fail:** 200–2,000 ms + paid LLM tokens on *every* inbound message; breaks Core's silent-without-LLM gate (`ARCHITECTURE.md:22, 75`). | Reads raw text (permitted in `crates/reason/`), but forces wake before gating. | **Reject for live turn arrival.** Reserve `crates/reason/` for **idle sleep consolidation** (`ModelPurpose::Consolidate`) to split/merge ambiguous `Provisional`/`Sealed` episodes once subsequent context (`SC`) exists. |
| **B. Neural Edge / `forward.rs` alone** | Feed raw `e_256` into `TabM` + `h_t` and hope implicit `64d` `h_t` separates threads. | `< 0.2 ms` (`forward.rs:605-645`). | Zero raw text. | **Insufficient alone:** A single `64d` `h_t` vector overwrites Thread A when Thread B bursts (`SocialMemBench` single-stream conflation), and `EpisodeBook` / `PLACE_TAIL` remain unthreaded. |
| **C. `episodes.rs` (`EpisodeBook`) + Embedding/Actor/$\Delta t$ Streaming Linker *(Recommended)*** | Replace single `active_by_place` slot with a bounded pool of **$M \le 4\text{–}8$ active thread slots per `(PlaceId, Audience)`**, scored online via `e_256` centroid cosine + actor overlap + `reply_to`/expectation + $\Delta t$ decay. | **`< 10 µs`** per percept ($M \le 8$ `256d` dot products = ~2,048 MACs); zero LLM calls. | **100% invariant-clean:** uses only `TaggedEmbedding` (`e_256`), `ActorId`, `EventId`, `Time`, `Audience`, and `Taint` — zero raw text, zero word lists. | **Primary Choice.** Unifies thread reconstruction, episodic boundaries, `x_num` thread features, and thread-aware `[PLACE_TAIL]`/`Recall` inside an existing Core primitive. |

#### Streaming Scoring Function (Pure Numerics & IDs in `crates/core/`)

Grounded in `CluCDD` (`arXiv:2302.08146`), `PERSIST`'s 3W joint scoring (`arXiv:2610.07725`), and existing [`EpisodeRecord.centroid`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/consolidation/episodes.rs#L68) + [`participant_jaccard`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/consolidation/episodes.rs#L49-L56):

Given an incoming percept $p$ at `(place, audience, now)` with embedding $e_{256}(p)$ (matched by `EmbeddingTag` partition!), sender $a = p.\text{actor}$, and optional explicit `reply_to`:
For each active open/provisional thread slot $k \in \{1 \dots M\}$ in `(place, audience)`:

1. **Hard link fast-path:** If `p.reply_to` (or `p.caused_by` / matched `OpenWait` / `ExpectationRecord`) points to an `EventId` inside thread $k$, assign directly to thread $k$ (deterministic structural anchor).
2. **3W Joint Affinity Score (`Who-What-When`):**
   $$S(p, k) = w_{\text{sem}} \cdot \cos\big(e_{256}(p),\, \mu_k\big) + w_{\text{who}} \cdot \text{actor\_affinity}(p, k) + w_{\text{when}} \cdot \exp\!\left(-\frac{\Delta t_k}{\tau_{\text{thread}}}\right)$$
   - **What ($\cos$):** `cosine_similarity` between $e_{256}(p)$ and thread $k$'s running unit centroid $\mu_k$ (already computed in [`episodes.rs#L429-L459`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/consolidation/episodes.rs#L429-L459); degrades cleanly to $0.0$ when `embeddings` is empty or tags mismatch).
   - **Who (`actor_affinity`):** Combination of $\mathbb{I}[a \in \text{participants}_k]$, recency of $a$'s last turn in thread $k$, and $\mathbb{I}[\text{addressees}(p) \cap \text{participants}_k \neq \emptyset]$.
   - **When ($\Delta t_k$):** Exponential decay with $\tau_{\text{thread}}$ (e.g., $180\text{–}600\text{ s}$), replacing the brittle 30-minute cliff (`DEFAULT_EPISODE_GAP_MS = 1_800_000`) when a second topic starts immediately ($\Delta t = 5\text{ s}$) with low cosine & disjoint actors!
3. **Assignment & Bounded Eviction:**
   - Assign $p$ to $k^* = \arg\max_k S(p, k)$ if $S(p, k^*) \ge \theta_{\text{join}}$ (tie-breaking deterministically by highest `last_event_id` / lowest `episode_id`); otherwise open a new thread slot.
   - If active slots exceed $M$ (e.g., $M = 6$), transition the oldest inactive slot (`(end_ms, episode_id)`) to `Provisional` (eligible for `merge_eligible_episodes` or sleep consolidation).
   - **Important `EmbeddingGemma 2` `256d` caveat (from Run 6 `R-66`):** Short 1–3 word messages (`"ok"`, `"gas"`, `"wkwk"`) or vectorless events have weak/zero `e_256` cosine. For very short bursts ($\Delta t < 5\text{ s}$) from an actor already active in thread $k$, `actor_affinity + exp(-Δt/τ)` naturally keeps *pecah balon* fragments attached to the actor's active thread without needing lexical rules!

### 2.2 Per-Participant Working State (`h_actor`) & State Factorization

Why per-place `h_t` (`64d`) alone breaks down in multi-party chat, and how 2026 literature informs a clean factorization:

1. **Diagnosis from 2026 Multi-Party Memory Literature:**
   - **`SpeakerMem-R1` (`arXiv:2609.26780`):** Proves dual-track separation (**group-level view** vs **person-level view**) is essential to prevent attribution errors and cross-talk in Slack/Discord-style rooms.
   - **`SocialMemBench` (`arXiv:2605.17789`):** Demonstrates that flat room memory suffers **single-stream conflation** (Alice's urgency/topic gets averaged with Bob's joke) and **norm-individual conflation** (a quiet participant who rarely speaks vs a chatty participant spamming the room look identical to a single room hidden state).
   - **`GroupMemBench` (`arXiv:2605.14498`):** Shows speaker-grounded belief tracking and audience-adapted language require per-speaker state; naive room pooling drops update accuracy to **27.1%**.
   - **`SinkState` (`arXiv:2611.14246`):** Shows linear-attention / Gated DeltaNet recurrent states naturally inhabit a low-rank subspace — meaning a **`16d` or `32d` `h_actor`** captures participant conversational momentum/stance without needing full `64d` per actor.

2. **Two Candidate Designs for Per-Participant Working State in Core:**

   - **Option 1 (Zero-Weight-Breaking Baseline — Deterministic Numeric Actor Features + Pairwise Matrix):**
     - Fix [`state/mod.rs#L720, L1747`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/state/mod.rs#L720) so `actor_exchanges` (or a room-scoped `place_actor_stats` LRU) tracks every participant even in `audience.len() > 2` rooms.
     - Track bounded per-`(PlaceId, ActorId)` counters + pairwise `(ActorA -> ActorB)` directed interaction traces (recency of reply/addressing between `sender` and `soul` / other room members) + `ActorCard.reliability_mean()` from [`ledger/mod.rs#L86-L93`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/ledger/mod.rs#L86-L93).
     - Requires **only** expanding `x_num` (schema v1 `18d` -> schema v2 `24d`–`26d`) or replacing low-leverage slots; zero new recurrent cell tensors.

   - **Option 2 (Full Factorized Recurrent Working Memory — `h_place (64d)` + `h_actor (16d–32d)`):**
     - Maintain `h_place: 64d` (room cadence/ambient floor state) **and** `h_actor: 16d` or `32d` (compact Gated DeltaNet-2 or minGRU state per active participant in that place/audience).
     - **Step semantics on percept from `actor` at `(place, audience)`:**
       1. Read `h_place_prev` (`64d`) and `h_actor_prev` (`d_actor`, e.g., `24d` or `32d`, zero-initialized for unseen actors).
       2. Modality-Fusion concat becomes `[e_proj, n_proj, h_place_prev, h_actor_prev]` -> `d_model`.
       3. After the `TabM` (`k=8`) trunk produces `h_mean`, step **two** decoupled `GatedDeltaNet2` (or `minGRU`) cells:
          - `h_place_next = step_memory_cell(place_cell, h_mean, h_place_prev)` (`64d`)
          - `h_actor_next = step_memory_cell(actor_cell, h_mean, h_actor_prev)` (`16d–32d`)
       4. *(Optional Thread Coupling)* If thread slot $k^*$ from `EpisodeBook` also carries a compact `h_thread` (`32d`) or simply exposes `thread_cos`, `thread_dt_s`, `thread_actor_share` in `x_num`, the controller simultaneously knows **what thread** (`h_thread` / `x_num`), **who is speaking** (`h_actor`), and **room climate** (`h_place`).
     - **Memory & Latency Cost:**
       - Per active actor in a room: `32 * 4 = 128 bytes` (`f32`). With a hard LRU cap of `K_ACTORS = 32` per place, total per-room actor memory is **4 KiB**!
       - Extra FLOPs in `forward_step`: for `d_model = 224, d_actor = 32` (`GatedDeltaNet2`), 3 linear layers of `[32, 224]` = `21,504` MACs (~`+2–5 µs` on CPU, well inside the `< 1 ms` budget; compare `forward.rs#L609` `1M` preset).

   - **Recommendation on Staged Validation:** Test **Option 1** (thread-disentanglement features + room-scoped actor/reliability/pairwise features in `x_num`) first as a clean ablation against **Option 2** (`h_actor` `16d–32d` recurrent state), because `ARCHITECTURE.md:93-94` mandates: *"A mechanism whose removal changes nothing measurable is removed."*
