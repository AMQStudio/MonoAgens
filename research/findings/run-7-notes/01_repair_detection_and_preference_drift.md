## 2. Deep Technical Synthesis

### 2A. Language-Agnostic Implicit Correction Detection (`p_user_correction`)

#### Why Lexical Heuristics Fail — and What Replaces Them
Per [`ARCHITECTURE.md#L85-L97`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/ARCHITECTURE.md#L85-L97), Core sees **only opaque IDs, numbers, and embeddings** and **never** reads raw bytes or word lists. In multilingual / Indonesian / code-switched group chat (`R-66`), lexical markers (`"bukan"`, `"no"`, `"actually"`, `"maksudku"`) fail anyway across *singkatan*, pro-drop ellipses, and *pecah balon* multi-bubble repairs. Moreover, `Reading Between the Turns` (`2026.luhme-1.3`) establishes that user repair is context-dependent: **rephrasing/repetition** after a deficient agent reply strongly indicates breakdown, whereas **follow-up elaboration** after a helpful reply reflects normal progress.

Therefore, `p_user_correction` in the `1M` Pure-Rust controller must infer repair **purely from geometric, temporal, structural, and recurrent-state cues** across the pair `(last_agent_reply, next_user_msg)` plus `user_prev_before_agent` and `Gated DeltaNet-2` `h_t`:

1. **Semantic Divergence / Misalignment Geometry (Language-Agnostic):**
   - `cos_agent_user`: normalized cosine similarity between `e_256(last_soul_msg)` and `e_256(next_user_msg)` (detects abrupt topic rejection or orthogonal pushback vs smooth continuation).
   - `cos_prev_user_rephrase`: cosine similarity between `e_256(user_turn_before_soul)` and `e_256(next_user_msg)` (`same_actor` or same topic). When `cos_prev_user_rephrase` is **high** while `cos_agent_user` is **low/moderate**, the user is *repeating or rephrasing their original request because the agent missed the point* (`2026.luhme-1.3` signature).
   - `cos_expected_reply_gap`: `1.0 - max_cos(e_256(next_user_msg), expected_reply_embeddings)` on any open `Response` expectation (`expectation_book.evaluate_response_content` already evaluates this in [`step.rs#L465-L470`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/turn/step.rs#L465-L470), currently only emitting a binary `R-18` `ContentMiss`).
   - **Why scalar cosine alone is insufficient:** In `256d` MRL `EmbeddingGemma 2` space, a polite correction (`"maksud saya jam 3, bukan jam 2"`) has **high** overall cosine (`~0.75–0.88`) with the agent's reply (`"oke, jam 2 ya"`) because all topic tokens overlap, while differing along negation/polarity subspaces! Hence we also analyze **projected pairwise difference/Hadamard features** (Option B below) or `h_t` residual surprise.
2. **Temporal & Structural Turn-Taking Features (All Post-`VisibleScope`):**
   - `has_recent_soul_reply`: `{0.0, 1.0}` indicating a visible soul message exists in `place` within horizon (e.g. `<= 3600 s`).
   - `dt_since_last_soul_act_s`: already computed as `x_num[6]` ([`features.rs#L95`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/neural/features.rs#L95)). Rapid user follow-up (`2–20 s`) right after a soul reply differs sharply from a new session hours later.
   - `reply_link_to_soul`: `{0.0, 1.0}` whether `percept.reply_to` points to `last_soul_event_id` (stronger than generic `is_reply_or_expect_link` in `x_num[2]`).
   - `is_same_target_actor`: `{0.0, 1.0}` whether `percept.actor` matches the actor the soul last addressed (`open_resp.people` or `last_soul_target`).
   - `user_intervening_burst_flag`: distinguishes multi-bubble *pecah balon* continuation (user sending 2nd bubble before soul spoke) from post-soul repair (`soul_acted_since_sender_last_act`).
3. **Recurrent Working Memory (`Gated DeltaNet-2` `h_t`):**
   - `Gated DeltaNet-2` (`arXiv:2605.22791`, implemented at [`forward.rs#L342-L362`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/neural/forward.rs#L342-L362)) tracks channel-wise erase/write context `h_t` across turns in `place_memory[place]`.
   - **Critical V2 Gap Discovered:** Currently, when the soul itself sends a message and its `SelfCaused` percept comes back, [`state/mod.rs#L624-L629`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/state/mod.rs#L624-L629) calls `record_self_caused_percept` instead of running `forward_step`, and `EventKind::Action` does **not** advance `place_memory[place]`! Thus `h_t` currently **never encodes what the soul just said**! Fixing this (stepping `h_t` or caching `last_soul_act_emb` in `PlaceHistoryEntry` / `MemoryEntry` strictly per `VisibleScope`) is essential for `h_t` to detect agent–user mismatch.

#### Closing Loop A & Loop B Without Over-Reacting (`arXiv:2604.19245`, `arXiv:2610.15154`, `arXiv:2603.23184`)
- **Loop A (Triggered Reasoning Repair / Clarification):**
  - When `p_user_correction >= theta_repair` (with `TabM` epistemic uncertainty `<= theta_epistemic_max`, avoiding "Second-Guesser" flailing per `arXiv:2604.19245`):
    - Gate elevates `Wake` (or annotates `DecisionTrace`) with structural repair cues: `repair_target_event_id = Some(last_soul_event_id)` and `rephrase_anchor_id = Some(prior_user_event_id)`.
    - Reasoning is woken with those `VisibleScope`-verified IDs in context, enabling **Loci-style selective repair** (`arXiv:2610.15154`) via `ThoughtAct::Revise` (`step.rs:1304`) or **GAVA-style** clarification/verification (`arXiv:2610.00282`).
    - **Security (`CAPTURE`, `arXiv:2609.02265`):** `Taint` governs authority. A correction from `Taint::Untrusted` cannot silently revise a `Taint::Trusted` or `Observed` belief; it triggers clarification (`ask`/`verify`) instead.
- **Loop B (Honest Delayed Negative Feedback into `PlaceAdaptState`):**
  - **High-Severity V2 Bug/Gap Found in `step.rs`:** At [`step.rs#L790-L793`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/turn/step.rs#L790-L793), `ReceiptOutcome::Done` **immediately** calls `record_place_adapt_feedback(core, &place_c, GateArm::Go, 0.5, false)`. If the soul spoke out of turn or misunderstood, the message delivered cleanly (`Done`), so `PlaceAdaptState` **wrongly rewarded `GateArm::Go` with `+0.5`** and **never corrected it later**! (Contrast `GateArm::Silent`, which already receives delayed one-sided `-0.5` / `+0.5` at [`step.rs#L3114`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/turn/step.rs#L3114), [`step.rs#L3268`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/turn/step.rs#L3268), and [`context_emit.rs#L1528`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/turn/context_emit.rs#L1528).)
  - **Proposed Fix:** When `p_user_correction >= theta_feedback` arrives on a user percept following a recent soul `Go` act (within the place's response horizon, with `is_same_target_actor` or `reply_link_to_soul`), emit a **continuous propensity-weighted delayed negative update** (`ImplicitRM` `arXiv:2603.23184`, `ImplicitFeedback` `arXiv:2507.23158`):
    $$r_{\text{corr}} = -\operatorname{clamp}\!\left(\frac{p_{\text{user\_correction}} - \theta_{\text{fb}}}{1 - \theta_{\text{fb}}},\, 0,\, 1\right), \quad \text{is\_delayed\_one\_sided} = \text{true}$$
    applied to `GateArm::Go` (with `Taint::Untrusted` guarded or down-weighted so adversarial strangers in public rooms cannot poison `place_adapt` logits, matching Run 6 `C-4`). Wait-and-Silent arms are left untouched; if `Go` was already prematurely rewarded `+0.5` on `ReceiptOutcome::Done`, either defer `Go`'s social reward to the `Response` expectation resolution window (`step.rs:465-537`) or apply `-1.0 * r_corr` so net reward accurately reflects social reception.

### 2B. Long-Term User Preference Drift Across Weeks/Months (Without Eroding Core Invariants)

Synthesizing `PAMU` (`arXiv:2510.09720`), `VARS` (`arXiv:2603.20939`), `PerMemBench` (`arXiv:2605.25535`), `MEMDRIFT` (`arXiv:2605.24941`), `CAPTURE` (`arXiv:2609.02265`), `CORE` (`arXiv:2609.12373`), and `MEMOIR` (`arXiv:2611.07631`):

1. **Three Distinct Timescales — Not One Decay Rate:**
   - **Fast / Workload Shift (hours–7 days, e.g., exam week):** User wants terse answers and fewer interjections this week. Handled by **discounted `PlaceAdaptState`** (fast half-life $\tau_{1/2} \approx 3\text{–}7\text{ days}$) + a **fast Beta shadow** (`lambda_fast ≈ 0.85/day`), leaving durable beliefs untouched unless sustained.
   - **Medium / Genuine Preference Drift (weeks–months):** Sustained style, topic, or convention changes confirmed across $\ge k$ episodes (`EpisodeBook` / sleep consolidation gating per `PerMemBench` and `CORE`). Handled by **`BeliefBook::conventions`** (`CONV_LAMBDA = 0.95/day`, `tau = 4.0`, [`credence.rs#L23-L24`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/beliefs/credence.rs#L23-L24)) and **`BeliefRecord` `BetaCredence`** (`CREDENCE_LAMBDA_DOMAIN = 0.98/day`, [`credence.rs#L16`](file:///home/muqsith/Projects/Mono/crates/core/src/beliefs/credence.rs#L16)), **strictly keyed by `(DomainId, PlaceId, Audience)`** to block `MEMDRIFT` cross-domain leakage (`arXiv:2605.24941`).
